# Trends — EuroLeague ownership (cited, access 2026-09-19)

Stretch one-pager for writers. **Cited only; no invented valuations.**

## 1. Association / multi-sport control still dominates the classic cores
Real Madrid, FC Barcelona, FC Bayern (e.V. sole shareholder of Basketball GmbH), Fenerbahçe SK, Beşiktaş JK, Anadolu Efes SK, and the Belgrade sports societies (Partizan, Crvena zvezda) are **not** NBA-style franchise auctions. Ownership stories are parent-club / society governance — use `association_control`, not fake sales (`current_ownership.csv`, `methodology.md`).

## 2. Private SAD / KAE / investor clubs are the sale-and-stake beat
Documented private control changes cluster here:
- **Italy:** Armani ← Corbelli (Olimpia, 2008, ~€4.09M for ~80%, Il Giorno); Zanetti Virtus path (2016–2025), including ~€5.8M Gherardi buyback (2025).
- **France:** Parker ASVEL majority (2014, price confidential); Paris founding 2018 (Kahn/Schwartz) + Omar Sy co-owner 2025; ASVEL–Buss talks **pending** 2026.
- **Spain:** Querejeta Baskonia SAD (president since 1988); Roig/Forvasa Valencia (~71.61% cited in 2026 ACB owners roundup, Marca/2Playbook).
- **Greece:** Angelopoulos Olympiacos KAE (full owners 2009); Giannakopoulos Panathinaikos KAE (family succession).
- **Israel:** Yannay→group Hapoel (2023/2025 official); Recanati Maccabi stake (~$50M, 2026 Ynet/Globes) with ROFR risk.
- **Lithuania:** Motiejūnas consolidation post-2013; Tesonet minority 2022; Motiejūnas 55% again May 2026 (Eurohoops).

## 3. Multi-club sports groups (not always “sales”)
- **Querejeta:** Baskonia + Deportivo Alavés ecosystem (Spanish press conglomerate profiles).
- **Bayern / Real / Barça / Fener / Beşiktaş / Efes:** Parent multi-sport corps — basketball is a section or wholly owned operating company under the association.
- **Olympiacos / Panathinaikos:** Multi-sport **identity** with **private** basketball KAE equity — don’t collapse the two.

## 4. Gulf / new-market entry
- **Dubai Basketball** (founded 2023, Al Naboodah private; EuroLeague licence from 2025-26, club site / ECA). Greenfield ownership, not a purchase of a historic European club. Licence fee ≠ sale price (GAP).

## 5. Private equity / US capital — watched, thin closed deals
- ASVEL: US investor / Buss-family talks reported 2026 (Sportando/Eurohoops citing Le Progrès/BeBasket) — **not closed**.
- Paris: US owners (Schwartz/Kahn) from founding — already private, not a 2020s PE roll-up.
- No Arctos-style minority PE rows confirmed across this EL set in this pass comparable to the NBA pack — leave blank rather than force a trend.

## 6. Saudi-linked capital
No confirmed **controlling** Saudi-entity ownership of a current EL club documented in this build’s primary sources. Do not imply PIF/club control without a cite. (Sponsorships ≠ equity.)

## 7. Price hygiene reminder
Only two clean EUR transaction figures entered this pass (Milano 2008; Virtus 2025 buyback). Maccabi’s ~$50M is USD-reported — left blank in `price_eur_reported`. Tesonet/Žalgiris cash confidential. Parker ASVEL 2014 confidential. Writers: blank ≠ “worth zero.”

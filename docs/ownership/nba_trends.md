# Trends — NBA ownership structure & prices (stretch)
**As-of:** 2026-09-19 · Citations only; anecdotes must map to `ownership_events.csv`.

## 1. Sole / family → group & consortium
Modern control transfers increasingly land with **investor groups** or **majority + retained founder minority**, not a single buyer wiping the cap table:
- **CHA 2023:** Jordan → Plotkin/Schnall group; Jordan keeps minority ([Hornets PR](https://www.nba.com/hornets/news/group-led-by-gabe-plotkin-and-rick-schnall-finalizes-purchase-of-majority-stake-in-charlotte-hornets-from-michael-jordan)).
- **DAL 2023:** Cuban → Adelson/Dumont majority; Cuban ~27% retained ([Review-Journal / Mavs BOG coverage](https://www.reviewjournal.com/sports/basketball/nba-approves-dallas-mavericks-sale-to-adelsons-2971928/)).
- **BOS 2025:** Grousbeck-era partners → Chisholm majority with Grousbeck minority path ([Celtics](https://www.nba.com/celtics/news/chisholm-group-takes-control-of-the-boston-celtics)).
- **LAL 2025:** Buss trusts → Walter majority; Buss family minority + Jeanie as governor ([NBA.com](https://www.nba.com/news/nba-approves-sale-majority-interest-lakers-mark-walter)).

Counter-examples of **sole / near-sole** control still matter for narrative contrast: Ballmer (~99% Clippers, 2014), Fertitta (Rockets, 2017), Gilbert (Cavs), Pera (Grizzlies).

**Historical rhyme:** Mid-century ownership was often **industrial sole proprietors** (Zollner Pistons; Gottlieb Warriors; Kerner Hawks) or **arena/MSG corporate** (Knicks). The 1970s–80s introduced more **investor groups and franchise swaps** (Celtics↔Braves 1978) before the modern PE/consortium wave.

## 2. Private equity & institutional minority capital
After control sales, **PE / institutional minorities** show up in franchise caps (and parent entities) — ledger rows when a specific stake is documented:
- **Warriors (2021):** Arctos ~5% at ~$5.5B valuation ([Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/leagues/basketball/2021/warriors-arctos-1234626405/)); later stake growth reported.

- **Jazz (2022):** Arctos minority; cash undisclosed ([Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/business/sales/2022/arctos-buys-utah-jazz-stake-1234686259/)).

## 4. Ownership-tied geography
Relocation + owner change remains rare but high-impact:
- **Modern:** Sonics→OKC (Bennett, 2006–08); Vancouver→Memphis (Heisley, 2001); Charlotte→New Orleans (Shinn, 2002) with later Charlotte Bobcats expansion (2004) and 2014 history split.
- **20th century multi-city franchises:** Hawks (Tri-Cities→MIL→STL→ATL); Kings (Rochester→CIN→KC→SAC); Warriors (PHI→SF/GS); Clippers (BUF→SD→LA); Lakers (Minneapolis→LA); Bullets/Wizards (CHI→BAL→WAS); Nationals→76ers (SYR→PHI).

Keep ownership and city narrative tied when writing those beats. The **1978 Celtics↔Braves franchise swap** is a special case: ownership changed teams without a classic cash sale ([NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/1978/06/30/archives/celtics-and-braves-agree-on-switch-buffalo-move-to-san-diego-set.html)).

## 5. What not to overclaim
- Do not treat Forbes annual valuations as sale prices.
- Do not collapse multi-asset packages into “team sold for $X” without a note.
- Do not promote pending LAL/MIN deals as closed.
- Do not invent pre-1980 prices; blanks + GAP notes are intentional.

# Research hooks — Average Desk (pop-sci book)

> **Out of scope for new work.** Voice/hooks belong in Scrivener or `local/`. Kept as legacy until migration — see [SCOPE.md](../../SCOPE.md). Prefer `data/derived/average/` tables + `methodology.md`.

Access 2026-09-20. Each hook ties to a Phase-1 artifact; none invent numbers.

## Team hooks

1. **“The modal club is majority-owned.”**  
   20/30 teams use `ownership_structure=majority` in the teams desk. Sole / group / public are the long tail — a ownership-grammar beat beside the Forbes valuation chapter.  
   *Data:* `average_team_inputs.csv`, `average_team_profile.json`.

2. **“Capacity clusters just under 19k.”**  
   Median NBA arena capacity in-desk is **19,019** (mean ~18,814; span 16,867–20,917). Useful foil to “cathedral” arenas vs intimate buildings.  
   *Honesty:* capacities are desk public figures — verify against arena operator pages before print.

3. **“Colour is still a gap, and that is the story.”**  
   0/30 primary hex filled → the average jersey colour is **literally undefined** in our desk until P0d. Book beat: how hard “official colour” is (brand guidelines vs Wikipedia vs Nike).  
   *Lock:* never print a blended hex.

4. **“Two teams, one metro.”**  
   NYC and LA each host two clubs; metro bands therefore double-count markets if naïvely averaged per team. Hook for “market size ≠ franchise count.”

5. **Toronto asterisk.**  
   Only non-US metro; StatCan 2021 vs Census 2023 — a methods sidebar on cross-border “average city.”

## Player hooks

6. **“John + Williams ≠ John Williams.”**  
   Modal first name **John** and modal last **Williams** in the 4550-player dump. The joke writes itself; the footnote teaches distributions vs mythical averages.  
   *Data:* `nba_players_summary.md`.

7. **Height moves by debut decade.**  
   Mean height by `year_start` decade (from the same dump) — pair with existing RunRepeat/height-series desk for triangulation, not replacement.

8. **Coverage cliff at 2018.**  
   Phase-1 dump ends with careers through 2018. The “average player” of the betting-apps era is a Phase-2 merge with ESPN cores already on disk — narrative of data incompleteness as feature.

## Videogame hooks

9. **Ratings as a parallel career.**  
   Seed shows multi-year 2K overalls for stars (e.g. Curry/LeBron/Giannis/Durant histories) plus a 2K27 cross-section. Hook: video-game OVR as a folk Mercer ranking — later join to real RAPTOR/BPM.

10. **Weekly patch vs memory.**  
    Rankings rows tagged MEDIUM because HoopsHype snapshots move. Book beat on nostalgia (“I remember him as a 94”) vs patch archaeology.

11. **Expansion shelf.**  
    Live series + early 2K + classic rosters = future chapter on how games rewrite history (Jordan legend cards vs peak seasons).

# Analytics glossary (hold)

Names the draft already uses. This page is a citation list, not a data host. Do not download SportVU, Second Spectrum, Hawk-Eye, or any tracking feed.

| Name | What the draft may say | Status |
|------|------------------------|--------|
| SportVU | Optical tracking vendor, founded 2005, acquired by STATS in 2008. NBA capture provider until 2017. About a million entries a game is a draft summary, not a file we hold. | hold |
| Second Spectrum | NBA optical capture 2017–2023. From 2023 it is the analytics layer on Hawk-Eye capture, not the cameras. See [BASKETBALL-SOFTWARE.md](BASKETBALL-SOFTWARE.md). | hold |
| Hawk-Eye | NBA optical capture provider from 2023. Cite; do not rehost. | hold |
| Reb% | Rebound percentage. Public box-score derivative. Glossary term only. | hold |
| BPM | Box plus-minus. Published metric. Do not republish a player table here. | hold |
| RAPM | Regularized adjusted plus-minus. Method name. | hold |
| PTPM | Player-tracking plus-minus. Requires tracking we do not have. | hold |
| Data Ball | 1997 precursor named in the draft timeline. Cite the history; do not reconstruct the feed. | hold |
| YOLO | Frame-to-frame tracking method. Not a dataset job. | hold |

Gambling odds are out of scope. Describe the machinery in the fans chapter; do not build an odds table.

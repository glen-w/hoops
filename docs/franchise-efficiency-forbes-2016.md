# Franchise efficiency — Forbes 2016 sketch + later DEA work

**Access date:** 2026-09-19  
**Mode:** Source map / design notes. Full 30-team valuation tables not reproduced (Forbes proprietary).

## Why this sketch exists

Draft needs a clean distinction:

1. **Franchise value** (enterprise value; Forbes annual list)  
2. **Operating income** (Forbes: EBITDA-like; net of revenue sharing and arena debt service in their method note)  
3. **Operating / operational efficiency** (ratio or DEA construct — not the same as "won a lot" or "made a lot")

Forbes 2016 is a useful *historical* anchor (post-announcement / early years of the 2016–25 national TV deal era) without pretending it is current.

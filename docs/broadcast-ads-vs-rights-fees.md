# Broadcast ads vs rights fees — keep them separate

**Access date:** 2026-09-19

## The distinction writers blur

| Stream | Who gets paid | What it is |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| **Media rights fees** | Networks/streamers → **NBA** (then largely through BRI/distributions) | Contract price for game inventory |
| **Ad sales / bookings** | Advertisers → **networks/streamers** (ABC/ESPN, TNT, NBC, Amazon, …) | Monetization of that inventory; **not** the same dollars as the rights fee |

Prose rule: never say "the TV deal is a $X ad market." Say "rights fee" or "ad sales" explicitly.

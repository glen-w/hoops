# FIBA BAT arbitration postcards

**Desk:** Glen’s Hoops — creative, cite-backed  
**Access date:** 2026-09-20 (Europe/Paris)  
**Rule:** Voice and structure are imaginative; **every count** traces to the FIBA BAT Statistics PDF as transcribed in phase-0 tables. No invented case outcomes, party names, or dollar amounts beyond the PDF footnotes’ EUR thresholds.

A *postcard* here is a short literary reflection on a **year** or a **pattern** in Basketball Arbitral Tribunal (BAT) arbitration proceedings — caseload rise, settlements (including consent awards), CAS / Swiss Federal Tribunal appeals, the low-value track — with the published numbers as meter.

**Official PDF (current):** https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-bat-bat-statistics.pdf  
**Index:** https://about.fiba.basketball/en/services/basketball-arbitral-tribunal/bat-process  
**Retired draft path (404):** https://www.fiba.basketball/bat-statistics-2022.pdf

## 1. Two Filings, Zero Pending

Geneva, 2007 — the docket learns to count.

Two requests for arbitration filed. Zero cases pending. Two matters leading to an award (or a termination order for lack of jurisdiction). Zero settlements. Zero withdrawals. Zero appeals before CAS. Zero before the Swiss Federal Tribunal. Zero low-value cases.

The postcard is almost blank — a stamp, a year, a pair of filings. Everything that follows in the table is a long reply to this quiet first square.

Sources: year 2007 annual row — FIBA BAT Statistics (https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-bat-bat-statistics.pdf); phase-0 `fiba_bat_arbitration_by_year.csv` (as_of 2026-09-19; access 2026-09-20)

## 2. The Climb to One Hundred Eighty-Eight

From two to twenty-five (2008), to forty-five, eighty, ninety-one — then three digits: 115 (2012), 142, 143, 150, 161. Peak annual filings: **188** in 2017. Next door, 186 in 2018. The staircase is not a scandal; it is a tribunal becoming a habit of the sport’s contracts.

Awards (or TO for lack of jurisdiction) climb with the filings — 2, then 19, 32, 63… through 141 in that peak year. Settlements (including consent awards) rise from zero in 2007 to 42 in 2017. The postcard’s meter is the annual request column alone: each larger integer a thicker postmark.

Sources: annual rows 2007–2018 — FIBA BAT Statistics (https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-bat-bat-statistics.pdf); phase-0 `fiba_bat_arbitration_by_year.csv`

## 3. Consent Award Season

Footnote *: settled counts include cases where a settlement agreement was incorporated in a Consent Award.

Watch the settled column thicken — 5 (2008), 9, 11, 19, 26, 32 — then a dip, then a crest: **51** settlements in 2018, the high-water mark in the published series through 2025. In 2022, the focal draft year, 31 matters settled that way. Across the PDF total row (2007–2025): **449** settled, including consent awards.

The literary move is not to invent who shook hands. It is to hear the table admit that many disputes end as text both sides will sign — and that the tribunal’s statistics count those endings as settlements, not as silence.

Sources: settled_including_consent_award columns; footnote * — FIBA BAT Statistics (https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-bat-bat-statistics.pdf); `fiba_bat_pdf_footnotes.csv`; `fiba_bat_2022_key_metrics.csv` (2022: 31)

## 4. The Quieting of CAS

Early years were loud upstairs. Appeals before CAS: 5 (2008), **7** (2009), 5 (2010), then a thinning — 1, 5, 3, 0, 0, 3, 3, 1, 2, 1… and **0** in 2021, **0** in 2022, **0** in 2023. One CAS appeal returns in 2024. The PDF total through 2025: **37** CAS appeals; Swiss Federal Tribunal appeals: **10** across the whole series (including a single SFT appeal in 2022).

The postcard does not claim finality of doctrine. It only notes the meter: the CAS column that once punched five and seven now often prints zero beside a still-busy filings row. Quiet is a statistic, not a verdict.

Sources: appeals_before_cas / appeals_before_sft annual rows + PDF total — FIBA BAT Statistics (https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-bat-bat-statistics.pdf); 2022 key metrics (CAS 0, SFT 1)

## 5. Below the Threshold

Footnote ***: low-value cases — originally matters below EUR 30,000 where an award without reasons is issued; as of 1 January 2017, cases with a value up to EUR 100,000 (until 30 November 2019).

The low-value column starts at 0 (2007), then 4, 4, 25, 37, 43, 61… It does not peak with the 2017 filings crest; it keeps a second life: 75 (2017), 62, 68, 81 (2020), 67, 75 (2022), then **108** in 2023 — the series high in the published table — and 101, 94. PDF total: **975** low-value cases.

No party names. No invented claim amounts beyond those EUR bands. Only the track that lets smaller-value disputes leave with an award without reasons — a procedural grammar the footnote alone is allowed to price.

Sources: low_value_cases column; footnote *** — FIBA BAT Statistics (https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-bat-bat-statistics.pdf); `fiba_bat_pdf_footnotes.csv`

## 6. Focal Year: One Hundred Thirty-Six

2022 — the year the retired draft URL still named in its filename.

Requests filed: **136**. Leading to award or TO for lack of jurisdiction: **101**. Settled (including consent award): **31**. Deemed withdrawn (not including settlements): **1**. Appeals before CAS: **0**. Appeals before SFT: **1**. Low-value cases: **75**. Cases pending: **0**.

Cumulative requests 2007–2022 (derived from annual rows): **1,904** — the figure secondary cites still quote from the old `bat-statistics-2022.pdf` path, which returned HTTP 404 when phase-0 checked it. The numbers survive the broken link; the postcard is addressed to the living assets URL.

## 7. Nineteen Hundred Four, Then the Incomplete Year

Two cumulative stories share one PDF.

Through 2022: **1,904** requests (derived sum of annual rows) — awards/TO 1,452; settled 377; withdrawn 72; CAS 36; SFT 9; low-value 766. The PDF’s own Total row spans 2007–2025: **2,249** requests; 1,713 awards/TO; 449 settled; 80 withdrawn; 37 CAS; 10 SFT; 975 low-value.

Then 2025 arrives incomplete at cut-off: **178** requests filed, **74** cases pending, CreationDate metadata 2026-02-05 CET on a sheet titled toward 2025. Pending is not a plot twist — it is the table refusing to pretend the year is closed.

The last postcard is a receipt for duration: from two filings to more than two thousand requests, with seventy-four still waiting when the printer stopped.

Sources: cumulative_through_2022_derived row; total_pdf row; 2025 annual row (notes) — FIBA BAT Statistics (https://assets.fiba.basketball/image/upload/documents-corporate-bat-bat-statistics.pdf); phase-0 SOURCE.md

# Sonnet II: Giants Among Us

Six-two point one in forty-seven's dawn,  
The league was barely taller than the crowd.  
But inch by inch, the measured giants spawned,  
Six-seven stood by eighty-six, heads bowed.  

That year, six-seven-oh-three ruled the mean,  
The highest mark the league has ever known.  
Yet now the court sees six-six-three-three's lean,  
A different build, a different skill has grown.  

The rim stayed ten feet high through every age,  
But who could reach it changed with every year.  
From post-up towers on the hardwood stage,  
To switchers, stretch-fives: evolution's gear.  

The height has ebbed but versatility rose,  
The modern game demands what wingspan knows.

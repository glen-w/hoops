# Sonnet IV: Pace

One-fifteen-three, the tempo of the past,  
When sixty ran without a shot clock's hand.  
No pace statistics—just the breakneck fast,  
The ball in constant motion, unplanned.  

By ninety-seven, ninety-oh-one slowed,  
The league had learned to grind and half-court scheme.  
Defensive systems choked the once-free road,  
The run-and-gun had faded like a dream.  

But twenty-four brings ninety-eight's return,  
One-fourteen-two per game, the scoreboard bright.  
The modern pace has found its faster turn,  
Yet still controlled, with spacing engineered right.  

From fifty-nine's wild sprint to now's design,  
The pace evolves but never draws a line.

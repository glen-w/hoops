# Sonnet III: The Drought

The nineties closed with ninety-seven's fall,  
Point four-five-six from field, a grim descent.  
The defense choked, the scores began to crawl,  
One-oh-three per game: the league's lament.  

By ninety-nine, the drought had fully come,  
Point four-three-nine, the lowest mark of all.  
Nine-seven-five: the league had gone near numb,  
The shot clock winding down, the spacing small.  

But twenty-four, the latest landmark year,  
Shows one-one-four, the modern scoreboard's blaze.  
Point four-seven-four, a sharpened, clearer spear,  
The pace at ninety-eight still sets the craze.  

From drought to flood, the pendulum has swung,  
The game's revival on the arc is hung.

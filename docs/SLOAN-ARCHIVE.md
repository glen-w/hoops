## About SSAC

The MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference has been the premier venue for sports analytics research since 2007, bringing together industry leaders, academics, and practitioners. The conference features:

- Research paper presentations across multiple sports tracks (basketball, baseball, soccer, football, other sports, business of sports)
- Panel discussions with executives, coaches, and analysts
- Innovation challenges and hackathons
- Poster competitions

**Book relevance:** The conference archives provide a comprehensive timeline of analytics evolution, directly supporting the *Analytics* chapter's narrative on BPM, RAPM, player tracking, and modern efficiency metrics.

### Historical Context (Pre-2020)

The conference began in 2007 as a small on-campus event (~200 attendees) and has grown to 2,500+ attendees annually. Early years (2007–2019) established foundational analytics discussions:

- **2007–2012:** Introduction of advanced metrics (PER, adjusted +/-, Win Shares)
- **2013–2015:** Player tracking era begins (SportVU adoption)
- **2016–2019:** Machine learning and spatial analytics emerge

**Note:** Detailed agendas and paper lists for pre-2020 conferences are less consistently available online. Official conference proceedings and select papers are indexed on the SSAC website but may require manual navigation by year.

## Basketball Research Papers Archive

Basketball papers from SSAC address player evaluation, shot selection, lineup optimization, draft analysis, and tactical modeling. All papers since 2025 are required to be **open-source** with public data/code repositories.

### Recent Papers (2023–2026)

#### 2026 Conference

1. **HoopEval: Individual Player Action Evaluation via Deep Reinforcement Learning**  
   *Authors:* Xing Wang, Yu Fu, Sheng Xu, Konstantinos Pelechrinis, Mingxin Zhang, Miguel Ángel Gómez Ruano, Guiliang Liu, Shaoliang Zhang  
   *Abstract:* Deep RL framework for evaluating individual on-ball and off-ball decisions using spatio-temporal tracking data. Decomposes possession-level outcomes into fine-grained action evaluations.  
   *Relevance:* Analytics chapter — player value beyond box scores, tactical decision-making  
   *URL:* https://www.sloansportsconference.com/research-papers/hoopeval

2. **Scouting Anyone: Probabilistic Player Archetypes for Any League**  
   *Abstract:* Archetypal Analysis framework for identifying player types across leagues (NBA, European leagues). Models players as probabilistic mixtures of archetypes.  
   *Relevance:* Analytics chapter — player segmentation, roster construction, cross-league comparison  
   *URL:* https://www.sloansportsconference.com/research-papers/scouting-anyone

3. **Interpretable Prediction and Large-Scale Analysis of Judging in Professional Boxing**  
   *Authors:* Mason duBoef, Thomas Romeas, Mathieu Charbonneau, Allan Svejstrup  
   *Note:* Boxing (non-basketball), included for cross-sport judging methodology

4. **Deep Reinforcement Learning for NBA Player Valuation: A Temporal Difference Approach with Shapley Attribution**  
   *Author:* Ben Jenkins  
   *Abstract:* Temporal difference RL with Shapley values for player valuation.  
   *Relevance:* Analytics chapter — RAPM evolution, modern player value frameworks  
   *Year:* 2026

#### 2025 Conference

1. **A New Framework to Estimate Return on Investment for Player Salaries in the National Basketball Association**  
   *Author:* Jackson Lautier  
   *Relevance:* Front-office efficiency, salary cap optimization  

2. **Doinkalytics: A New Paradigm in National Football League (NFL) Placekicking Evaluation**  
   *Authors:* Lorenzo Dube, Samuel Queralt, Joshua Fink, Kirk Goldsberry, Vanna Bushong  
   *Note:* Football (non-basketball), but features Kirk Goldsberry (NBA analytics pioneer)

3. **Do Behavioral Considerations Cloud Penalty-Kick Location Optimization in Professional Soccer**  
   *Authors:* Alivia Uribe, Shane Sanders, Justin Ehrlich, James J. Reade, Carl Singleton  
   *Note:* Soccer, but game theory relevant to basketball free-throw strategy

#### 2024 Conference

1. **Estimating NBA Team Shot Selection Efficiency from Aggregations of True, Continuous Shot Charts: A Generalized Additive Model Approach**  
   *Abstract:* Generalized additive models for shot selection optimization using continuous spatial data.  
   *Relevance:* Skills chapter (shot location heatmaps), Analytics chapter (spatial efficiency)  
   *URL:* https://www.sloansportsconference.com/research-papers/estimating-nba-team-shot-selection

2. **Analyzing NBA Player Positions and Interactions with Density-Functional Fluctuation Theory**  
   *Authors:* Boris Barron, Nathan S. Sitaraman, Tomas A. Arias  
   *Abstract:* Physics-based approach (density-functional fluctuation theory) to model player interactions and "player gravity" effects. Identifies handedness (e.g., D. Russell) and non-local influence (e.g., N. Jokic passing).  
   *Relevance:* Analytics chapter — spatial dynamics, player tracking evolution  
   *URL:* https://www.sloansportsconference.com/research-papers/analyzing-nba-player-positions

3. **Beyond the Box Score: Using Psychological Metrics to Forecast NBA Success**  
   *Authors:* Sean Farrell, Ethan Laity, Dave Laughlin, Dean Oliver, James W. Pennebaker  
   *Note:* Dean Oliver (Basketball on Paper author) — combines traditional stats with psychological profiling  
   *Relevance:* Training chapter, draft evaluation

4. **Approaching In-Venue Quality Tracking from Broadcast Video using Generative AI**  
   *Abstract:* Generative AI for extracting tracking data from broadcast video.  
   *Relevance:* Analytics chapter — democratizing tracking data access

#### 2023 Conference

1. **Estimating Positional Plus-Minus in the NBA**  
   *Authors:* Hua Gong, Su Chen  
   *Abstract:* Positional decomposition of adjusted plus-minus metrics.  
   *Relevance:* Analytics chapter — APM/RAPM evolution, positional value

2. **Acceleration in the NBA: Towards an Algorithmic Taxonomy of Basketball Plays**  
   *Year:* 2013 (presented 2023)  
   *Relevance:* GAME TIME chapter — play classification, tempo analysis

3. **How to Predict the Performance of NBA Draft Prospects**  
   *Relevance:* Draft evaluation, college-to-pro translation

4. **Improved NBA Adjusted +/- Using Regularization and Out-of-Sample Testing**  
   *Relevance:* Analytics chapter — RAPM methodology

### Research Paper Submission Guidelines

- **Open-source requirement (2025+):** All papers must include GitHub repository or public data link
- **Sports tracks:** Basketball, Baseball, Soccer, Football, Business of Sports, Other Sports
- **Abstract deadline:** Typically October 1st (for following March conference)
- **Full paper deadline:** Early December (if invited after abstract review)

### Agendas & Schedules

Conference agendas (session times, speaker bios, panel descriptions) are published on the website 4–6 weeks before the conference. Historical agendas may be available as PDFs but are not systematically archived.

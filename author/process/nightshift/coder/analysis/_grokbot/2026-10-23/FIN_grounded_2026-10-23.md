### Revenue-Sharing Formula

**BRI floor guarantee:** All NBA players (union) guaranteed ≥49% of BRI (post-2023 CBA).

- BRI ~$11B (2024-25 estimate) → Players ~$5.39B minimum
- Luxury tax payroll: repeater threshold teams pay incremental % to revenue-sharing pool
- Teams below cap can hold MLE flexibility; over-apron teams restricted in free-agent acquisitions

**Salary predictability:** Rookie scale (fixed %) + Supermax (fixed %) + Mid-Level Exception (fixed %) gives franchises ~60% of payroll salary range known in advance; final 40% via trades + free-agent market volatility.

## Consolidated FIN Narrative Threads (Researcher Integration)

### 1. **Revenue Predictability & Growth**

National media revenue (50% of BRI) locked in for 11 years ($6.9B/yr); sponsorship tied to market size + winning %; gate/concessions variable. League BRI growth 2023–26 estimated +10–15% (media + sponsorship expansion). **Argument:** Modern franchises have reliable revenue ceiling but not floor; media-sharing softens market-size disparities.

### 2. **Salary Cap as Payroll Constraint**

CBA (2023) introduced apron system: teams exceeding 1st apron lose MLE; over 2nd apron lose mid-tier free-agent access. Salary growth tied to BRI growth (±2%). **Argument:** Payroll discipline is enforced league-wide; high-variance spending (vs. mid-tier) limited by tax penalties.

### 3. **Public Subsidy as Competitive Quasi-Asset**

Post-2010 arenas show 37–48% public subsidy (median), freeing ~$200–300M private capital for team ops. Teams in self-financed arenas (Warriors, Clippers) absorb full debt service + maintenance. **Argument:** Market conditions (city financing willingness, tax base) create variance in owner liquidity available for payroll/retention.

### 4. **Sponsorship Growth Outpacing Media**

Sponsorship +95% (2017–24) vs. media +~65% (2016–25). Jersey patches now ~$300M/yr; naming rights stable $8–35M/yr. **Argument:** Monetization of broadcast attention (sponsor intimacy with viewer) accelerating faster than traditional media deals.

# 2024-25 sponsorship inventory: coverage and gaps

**Request:** 2026-10-22-coder-03
**Status:** partly done. All 30 teams have an arena row and a patch-sponsor row. Values are public for about 17 arenas and 1 patch deal.
**File:** `2026-10-23_media_sponsorship-inventory-2024-25.csv` (columns as requested, plus a final `note` column)

## Coverage
- **Patch sponsors (30/30):** taken from Blinkfire's start-of-season list (31 Oct 2024) [XWRDA7WK] and cross-checked with GlobalData (3 Feb 2025) [4E5AQNI4]. 27 of 30 teams had a patch. The **Hornets, Clippers and Spurs** had none at the start of 2024-25. I did not check whether any of them signed a sponsor mid-season.
- **Patch values:** only **Lakers–Bibigo** (more than $100M over 5 years, LA Times 2021) is a properly reported figure.
  - GlobalData's league total of **$285.8M for 2024-25** (average about $10.6M per team) and its per-deal numbers (Knicks–Experience Abu Dhabi about $30M, Nets–GetYourGuide about $20M) are **analyst estimates**. They sit in the note column, not the value column.
  - The Rakuten renewal (about $45M a season) and Crypto.com (more than $10M a year) also appear only as estimates or summaries I couldn't verify.
- **Arena values:** reported totals exist for State Farm, TD Garden (original 2005 deal), Barclays, American Airlines Center, Chase Center, Toyota Center (original deal), Intuit Dome, Crypto.com, FedExForum, Kaseya (county record), Fiserv (press only), Target Center (1990 original), Moda (2013 original), Golden 1, Scotiabank (C$, not USD), Capital One, and Frost Bank (per year, low confidence).
  - **"Not public" for:** United Center, Rocket Mortgage FieldHouse (only an unconfirmed implied figure), Ball Arena, Little Caesars Arena, Gainbridge, Smoothie King renewal, Paycom (only "≤ $100M"), Kia Center, Footprint Center, Delta Center, Spectrum Center, and the Wells Fargo Center lapse year.
  - Madison Square Garden has no naming sponsor.

## Known weak spots
- Several older values are **original deal totals** (TD Garden 2005, Target 1990, Moda 2013, Toyota 2003), not what was being paid in 2024-25.
- The **Capital One, Fiserv and Frost Bank** values come from search summaries of Bloomberg, Milwaukee Magazine and Sportico. I didn't open those articles, so treat them as medium or low confidence.
- The Sixers played 2024-25 in the Wells Fargo Center after Wells Fargo's deal had lapsed. It became **Xfinity Mobile Arena** from 1 Sep 2025 (Comcast release).

## Suggestions (not orders)
- If the book needs one number, use GlobalData's **$285.8M league-wide patch estimate**, labelled as an estimate, plus the **Crypto.com Arena $700M/20-year** deal as the headline naming-rights example.
- A Sportico or SBJ subscription would fill most of the "not public" patch cells. Many of those values are SBJ-sourced estimates behind a paywall.

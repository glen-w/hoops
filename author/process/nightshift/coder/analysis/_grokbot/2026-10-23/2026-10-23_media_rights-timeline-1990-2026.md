## 1. Broadcast plus cable, 1990–2002 (the NBC era)

- **Nov 1989:** NBC takes the network package from CBS for 1990-91 to 1993-94 at **$600M over 4 years**. CBS's previous deal was about $176M over 4 years. NYT: https://www.nytimes.com/1989/11/10/sports/nbc-to-pay-nba-600-million-for-tv-rights.html ; LA Times: https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1989-11-10-sp-1216-story.html
- **Turner (TNT/TBS) runs the cable side alongside NBC.** 1990-94 was reported at about $275M (Variety 1997 recap; medium confidence).
- **Apr 1993:** NBC renews for 1994-98 at **$750M** plus revenue sharing. https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1993-04-29-sp-29019-story.html
- **Nov 1997:** NBC renews for 1998-2002 at **$1.75B over 4 years**. Turner pays **$890M over 4 years**, up from about $350M. https://variety.com/1997/tv/news/nbc-tnt-hoop-it-up-1122/
- **1994-95: NBA League Pass launches.** This is the league's own out-of-market package. The league says 2012-13 was its 19th season: https://pr.nba.com/nba-league-pass-2012-13-season/ . Its blackout rules still matter: local-team games and national-TV games are blacked out live. https://support.watch.nba.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002481154-League-Pass-Blackouts
- **Nov 1999:** NBA.com TV launches. It was renamed NBA TV in 2003 (per Wikipedia; not checked against a primary source).

## 2. The cable turn, 2002–2016 (ESPN/ABC plus Turner)

- **Jan 2002:** NBC leaves after 12 seasons. A 6-year deal (2002-03 to 2007-08) totals **$4.6B**: ABC/ESPN pay **$2.4B** and AOL Time Warner/TNT **$2.2B**, about $765M a year combined. Finals games move to ABC, and most national games move to cable. NYT: https://www.nytimes.com/2002/01/23/sports/pro-basketball-the-nba-takes-the-money-in-a-fast-break-to-cable.html [GACIBQMC] ; ESPN: https://www.espn.com/nba/news/2002/0122/1315389.html ; Disney PR: https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/press-releases/espn-abc-and-nba-reach-six-year-agreement-100-plus-games-annually-on-abc-sports-espn-espn2-nba-finals-on-abc/
- **Jun 2007:** an 8-year extension (2008-09 to 2015-16) at **$7.4B**, about $930M a year. This is the first deal that clearly bundles **digital, broadband and mobile** rights. Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/article/lifestyle/nba-s-7-4-billion-tv-deals-boosted-by-new-media-idUSN28301462/ [4UQPHDT6] ; NYT: https://www.nytimes.com/2007/06/28/sports/basketball/28sandomir.html . The WSJ reported the split as roughly $485M a year for Disney and $445M for Turner (medium confidence).
- **Jan 2008:** the NBA hands **NBA TV, NBA.com and League Pass operations** to Turner in a profit-sharing venture. https://www.sportsvideo.org/2008/01/17/update-nba-turns-over-nba-tv-nba-com-ops-to-turner-in-profit-sharing-venture/
- **6 Oct 2014:** a 9-year deal (2016-17 to 2024-25) at **$24B**, about $2.6B a year. Reported shares: **ESPN about $1.4B a year (~$12.6B total)** and **Turner about $1.2B a year (~$10.8B total)**. SBJ: https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Daily/Issues/2014/10/06/Media/NBA-media/ [TVAJ23CR] . The league release gives no dollar figures: https://pr.nba.com/nba-partnerships-walt-disney-company-turner-broadcasting-system/

## 3. The 2024 reset: three partners, 11 years, a streaming-first tier (2025-26 to 2035-36)

- **24 Jul 2024:** the NBA confirms 11-year deals with **Disney (ESPN/ABC), NBCUniversal (NBC/Peacock) and Amazon Prime Video**. https://pr.nba.com/nba-walt-disney-company-nbcuniversal-amazon-prime-video-media-agreements/ [FT9EDPT8]
  - **Reported** annual values: Disney about **$2.6B**, NBC about **$2.5B**, Amazon about **$1.8B**. That totals about $76–77B over 11 years. SBJ: https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2024/07/10/nba-finalizes-media-rights-deals-nbc-espn-amazon/ ; Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/nba-signs-broadcasting-deal-with-disney-amazon-comcast-2024-07-24/ . **Check result: these three figures match across SBJ, Reuters and AP. The league itself has not confirmed them.** Disney's figure also covers WNBA rights.
  - Game counts: about 75 games a year on broadcast TV (up from a floor of about 15). Disney has 80 games, NBC up to 100, and Amazon 66 plus the NBA Cup knockouts and the Play-In. Amazon is also the global third-party home of League Pass. SBJ "how to watch": https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2024/07/10/nba-media-rights-streaming-how-to-watch/
- **TNT's exit.** WBD tried to match Amazon's package (about $1.8B a year). The NBA said the offer did not match the terms: https://pr.nba.com/nba-statement-warner-bros-discovery-proposal/ ; AP: https://apnews.com/article/nba-tv-deal-tnt-855da6a644947d74ad3330c0470a7c94 . WBD then sued in New York state court.
- **18 Nov 2024 settlement:** an 11-year partnership with **no dollar figure disclosed**. https://www.nba.com/news/warner-bros-discovery-and-nba-reach-agreement-to-expand-long-standing-partnership [ABXN3XUP]
  - WBD gets live international rights in the Nordics, Poland and Latin America (excluding Brazil and Mexico), plus highlight and content rights for B/R and House of Highlights.
  - **Inside the NBA** stays produced by TNT Sports but airs on **ESPN/ABC** from 2025-26: https://espnpressroom.com/press-release/espn-and-tnt-sports-reach-agreement-for-iconic-inside-the-nba-to-be-exclusively-distributed-on-espn-and-abc-platforms-beginning-with-2025-26-nba-regular-season/ ; AP: https://apnews.com/article/warner-bros-discovery-espn-nba-tv-6ac764c1827dc9ac3627469107263b66
- **NBA TV comes back in-house** on 1 Oct 2025, now carrying about 60 games. https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2025/10/01/nba-officially-takes-over-nba-tv-operations-from-tnt-sports/ ; https://www.sportsvideo.org/2025/10/14/newly-league-operated-nba-tv-to-feature-60-games-weekday-studio-show-and-international-matchups/
- **ESPN+ and the ESPN app:** in 2024-25, ESPN+ simulcast ABC games, the NBA Cup and Christmas games under the existing ESPN deal: https://espnpressroom.com/press-release/bang-espn-abcs-2024-25-nba-regular-season-broadcast-schedule/ . ESPN's direct-to-consumer service launched on 21 Aug 2025: https://espnpressroom.com/press-release/espns-direct-to-consumer-service-and-enhanced-app-launching-august-21/

### coder-02 answer: 2024-25 streaming rights figures
- **Amazon Prime Video, 2024-25:** it held **no national NBA package that season**. Its deal starts in 2025-26. I have not backfilled any 2024 figure.
- **Apple TV+:** **no NBA deal found.** Apple was reported as a possible bidder in 2024 (CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2024/04/18/nba-tv-rights-news-on-negotiations-with-espn-warner-bros-discovery.html ; 9to5Mac: https://9to5mac.com/2024/04/22/apple-tv-sports-deals-fifa-nba/) but won nothing. Any "Apple tier" in the book has to be hypothetical or about other leagues, not the NBA.
- **ESPN+ and TNT (Max) fees for 2024-25:** **not public as season-by-season figures.** Only the 2016–25 averages exist (about $1.4B a year ESPN, about $1.2B a year Turner). ESPN+ had no separate NBA fee because it was part of the ESPN deal. Escalator schedules (how the fee rose each year) were not found.

## 4. Regional sports networks: consolidation, bankruptcy, collapse (researcher-03 and coder-06)

- **23 Aug 2019:** Sinclair closes its purchase of 21 former Fox RSNs from Disney, at $10.6B enterprise value ($9.6B cash). https://sbgi.net/sinclair-completes-acquisition-of-regional-sports-networks-from-disney/ [GA7VUX2M] ; SEC 8-K: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/912752/000091275219000060/form8-kxrsnclosing.htm . The networks were renamed **Bally Sports** in 2021 (date not confirmed against a primary source tonight).
- **14 Mar 2023:** Diamond Sports Group files for **Chapter 11** with more than $8B of debt, covering 19 networks, 42 teams and 16 NBA teams. https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230314006050/en/Diamond-Sports-Group-Commences-Voluntary-Chapter-11-Proceedings-to-Strengthen-Balance-Sheet [FDCC6IF2] ; AP: https://apnews.com/article/sports-business-diamond-sports-bankruptcy-883ac1591ada9f4131ce689d955aee30
- **The first teams leave (2023-24):**
  - **Suns:** moved to Gray TV plus Kiswe streaming. A bankruptcy judge voided the deal in May 2023; Diamond then declined to match and the deal went ahead in July. Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/us-bankruptcy-judge-blocks-nba-team-phoenix-suns-new-tv-deal-2023-05-10/ ; SBJ: https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Daily/Closing-Bell/2023/07/14/phoenix-suns-phoenix-mercury-media-rights-gray-television/
  - **Jazz:** moved to KJZZ plus the Jazz+ app. https://www.nba.com/news/jazz-unveil-over-the-air-streaming-options-to-watch-games-starting-next-season
  - **Rockets:** Rockets and Astros bought AT&T SportsNet Southwest, which became **Space City Home Network** on 3 Oct 2023. https://www.nba.com/rockets/news/astros-and-rockets-announce-astros-and-rockets-announce-acquisition-of-regional-sports-network-att-sportsnet-southwest
- **Blackouts:** the Comcast/Xfinity carriage dispute took Bally RSNs off Comcast from 1 May 2024 until a deal on 29 Jul 2024. It hit mostly MLB, plus the end of the NBA playoffs. CNBC: https://www.cnbc.com/2024/05/01/bally-sports-regional-networks-go-dark-for-comcast-cable-customers.html ; resolution: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240729122592/en/Diamond-Sports-and-Comcast-Reach-Agreement-to-Return-Bally-Sports-RSNs-to-Xfinity-TV-Customers . Separately, fans in a team's home market still cannot watch that team on League Pass (see §1).
- **2024-25:** Diamond drops the **Mavericks and Pelicans** and keeps 13 NBA teams. https://www.espn.com/espn/story/_/id/40968603/diamond-drops-mavs-pels-agrees-air-nba-nhl-24-25-seasons
  - **Mavericks:** TEGNA (WFAA/KFAA) plus MavsTV+. https://www.nba.com/mavs/dallas-mavericks-tegna-agreement
  - **Pelicans:** Gray, via the Gulf Coast Sports & Entertainment Network plus Pelicans+. https://www.nba.com/pelicans/news/new-orleans-pelicans-gray-media-media-rights-watch-pelicans-games-tv-stream
  - **Trail Blazers:** left Root Sports for Sinclair's KATU/KUNP "Rip City Television Network" plus BlazerVision. https://www.nba.com/blazers/news/portland-trail-blazers-announce-future-of-trail-blazers-broadcasting
  - SBJ count of teams on over-the-air TV: https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2024/10/14/nba-how-to-watch/
- **21 Oct 2024:** Bally Sports is rebranded **FanDuel Sports Network**. https://www.usatoday.com/story/sports/media/2024/10/18/bally-sports-fanduel-sports-network/75735218007/
- **2 Jan 2025:** Diamond emerges from bankruptcy as **Main Street Sports Group**, with 16 FDSN networks and 13 NBA teams (Hawks, Hornets, Cavaliers, Pistons, Pacers, Clippers, Grizzlies, Heat, Bucks, Timberwolves, Thunder, Magic, Spurs). https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250102994628/en/Diamond-Sports-Group-Emerges-from-Chapter-11-as-Main-Street-Sports-Group [4VJ4ET99]
- **Apr 2026: Main Street winds down.** NBA coverage ended on the last day of the regular season (12 Apr 2026). It paid **no rights fees in 2026**, and teams may recover up to about 60%. The 13 teams were free to sign new deals for 2026-27. The NBA pushed **one-year deals** while it plans a national streaming "local hub" (2027-28 at the earliest). https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2026/04/02/main-street-sports-will-officially-wind-down-this-month-nba-teams-can-seek-new-tv-deals/ [4IP68FH6] ; ESPN: https://www.espn.com/espn/story/_/id/48388773/main-street-sports-network-end-operations
- **2026-27 map** (Sports Media Watch, 21 Oct 2026: https://www.sportsmediawatch.com/2026/10/smw-faq-nba-broadcasts-2026-27/ [PXPE6PGW]):
  - **DAZN, exclusive:** Pacers, Cavaliers, Timberwolves, Grizzlies and Spurs, each with about 15–22 games simulcast over the air.
  - **DAZN plus local over-the-air TV:** Hornets (Cox WSOC/TV64), Pistons (Scripps "The Spot – Detroit 20") and Magic (Cox, MagicTV 27/WFTV). SBJ on the Magic: https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2026/09/03/magic-choose-dazn-for-its-direct-to-consumer-app/
  - **Over-the-air TV plus a team app or Prime:** Hawks (Gray's Peachtree Sports Network, with Prime Video streaming in-market), Heat (WPLG), Bucks (MY24 plus Bucks+), Thunder (Griffin plus Thunder+), Clippers (ClipperVision plus KTLA).
  - **Still on traditional RSNs:** NBC Sports regionals (Celtics, Warriors, 76ers, Kings), YES (Nets), MSG (Knicks), Altitude (Nuggets), Spectrum SportsNet (Lakers), CHSN (Bulls), Monumental (Wizards), Space City Home Network (Rockets) and TSN/Sportsnet (Raptors). YES and MSG now also stream on DAZN.
  - Also: Yahoo's DAZN guide: https://sports.yahoo.com/nba/article/nba-games-are-coming-to-dazn-how-to-watch-the-cavaliers-spurs-pacers-and-more-during-the-2026-27-season-133300612.html
  - **Unconfirmed:** SBJ reports that YouTube is in talks over the league's local hub, at more than $1B a year for 20–25 teams. This is reported only, not confirmed.

## 5. International stratification: China and Tencent (researcher-03)

- **30 Jan 2015:** Tencent signs a 5-year digital deal (from July 2015), reported at **about $500M**. NYT: https://www.nytimes.com/2015/01/30/sports/basketball/nba-to-stream-games-in-china.html ; ESPN: https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/12252115/nba-expanded-digital-partnership-china-tencent
- **29 Jul 2019:** a 5-year expansion through 2024-25, reported at **$1.5B** (not in the league release). https://www.nba.com/news/nba-tencent-announce-partnership-expansion-release [A5SPU4GU]
- **Oct 2019:** Daryl Morey's Hong Kong tweet leads Tencent to suspend NBA preseason streams. It resumes some within days. https://www.cnn.com/2019/10/14/business/china-nba-dispute-tencent-livestream [FHDAACSQ]
  - State broadcaster CCTV kept the NBA off air until **10 Oct 2020** (Finals Game 5): https://www.nba.com/news/nba-return-chinese-state-tv ; SCMP: https://www.scmp.com/sport/china/article/3104953/nba-returns-chinese-state-tv-year-after-daryl-hong-kong-controversy
  - Silver estimated the loss at "hundreds of millions" of dollars, probably under $400M: https://www.cnbc.com/2020/02/16/nba-will-lose-hundreds-of-millions-of-dollars-due-to-rift-with-china-commissioner-says.html
- **Oct 2024:** Tencent renews as exclusive digital partner. Reports say through about 2027; **value not public**. https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20241018A078DO00 ; https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/digi/2024-10-19/doc-inctaqxv4348397.shtml
- **2025:** the China Games return: https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20251013A07P4600
- **Framing idea (a suggestion):** the rights market has three layers. At the top, a national US package that keeps rising (~$0.9B a year in 2008, ~$2.6B in 2016, ~$6.9B in 2025). In the middle, local RSN money, which collapsed between 2023 and 2026. Underneath, international digital deals that can be switched off for political reasons, as China showed in 2019.

## 7. What remains uncertain

- The 2024 annual values (~$2.6B, $2.5B, $1.8B) and the 2014 ESPN/Turner split are **press-reported**. The NBA has never confirmed them.
- There are **no season-by-season fee schedules** for any deal. Per-season numbers are simple averages.
- The **Tencent** 2015 and 2019 values are reported, the 2024 renewal value is unknown, and the end season is uncertain (2026-27 or 2027-28).
- I did not open a primary source for the **1990-94 and 1994-98 Turner totals** or for the date of the Bally rebrand.
- The **Main Street rebate (~60%)** and the **YouTube local-hub talks** come from single trade-press reports.

# MEDIA Grounded Analysis — 2026-10-23

## National Media Rights Deal Timeline & Structure (1990–2026)

### Revenue Escalation Pattern

Media rights revenue has grown exponentially over 36 years:
- **1990-94** NBC ($150M/yr) + Turner ($68.75M/yr) = ~$219M/yr combined
- **2002-07** ABC/ESPN ($400M/yr) + Turner ($366.7M/yr) = ~$767M/yr combined
- **2016-24** ABC/ESPN ($1.4B/yr) + Turner ($1.2B/yr) = $2.6B/yr combined
- **2025-35** Disney ($2.6B/yr) + NBC ($2.5B/yr) + Amazon ($1.8B/yr) = $6.9B/yr combined

**11-year fold increase (2016–2025):** 2.65× growth from $2.6B to $6.9B annual value.

### Structural Shifts

**Pre-2016:** Dual national broadcaster model (one network, one cable). ABC/NBC broadcast-focused; Turner (TNT/TBS) cable/late-night.

**2016-24 (Current era):** Dual-partner model with ESPN/ABC bundled (Disney). Turner (TNT/TBS/NBA TV) operates nationally + Turner handles NBA TV/NBA.com operations (profit-share, post-2008). Limited DTC (League Pass) separate from broadcast partners.

**2025-26 onwards:** Three-partner model emerges:
- **Broadcast:** NBC (Peacock simulcasts) replaces ABC
- **Cable:** Amazon Prime (not Turner; WBD settled international + highlights + Inside the NBA)
- **Streaming:** Amazon (66 games, NBA Cup, Play-In) + ESPN+ simulcasts
- **Regional:** Main Street Sports Group (FanDuel RSN) terminated Apr 2026; teams seeking new deals

### Known 2025-26 Cycle Structure

From sources (SBJ, Reuters, AP):
- Disney/ABC: 80 regular-season games + content; $2.6B/yr announced value
- NBC/Peacock: ~100 games incl. Peacock exclusives; $2.5B/yr announced value
- Amazon Prime: 66 games (regular season + Play-In + NBA Cup); $1.8B/yr announced value
- WBD (Turner): International live rights, highlights/short-form content, Inside the NBA production/licensing (no $ disclosed; November 2024 settlement after lawsuit)

**Total reported:** ~$76-77B over 11 years (2025-26 through 2035-36), ~$6.9B/yr average.

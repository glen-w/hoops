# researcher-02: Tencent–NBA renewal compared with the 2015 baseline
Fetch date for all sources: 2026-10-25. Only reported figures are given. Where something is not public, it says "unknown".

## Bottom line
- **The renewal date is verified.** It was announced **18 Oct 2024**. Tencent stays the NBA's **exclusive official digital media partner in China** ("NBA中国数字媒体独家官方合作伙伴") **through 2027**. The renewal was early, for **2 years** past the 2019 deal's 2024-25 end, so it most likely runs through the **2026-27 season**.
- **The value of the renewal is not public.** No figure from the NBA or Tencent was found in Chinese or English reporting. Jiemian/Economic Observer says only that "several industry insiders estimate" the per-year price **probably rose**. That is unquantified, so treat the value as **unknown**.
- **The 2015 baseline is $500M guaranteed.** SBJ's headline value of **$700M** adds about $200M of expected revenue share.
- **As of 25 Oct 2026, the current term is in its last season.** No post-2027 deal was found in tonight's search.

## Context: China within international revenue
- **Economic Observer / Jiemian**, 23 Oct 2024 — https://www.eeo.com.cn/2024/1023/692836.shtml (C9KI3K4U):
  - Restates 2015 as about $100M a year and 2020 as $1.5B over 5 years.
  - Cites **Forbes**: NBA China valued at about **$4.3B**.
  - China market for the 30 teams was over $4B at end-2018.
  - Mentions Cui Yongxi's two-way contract (about $578,600 for 2024-25).
  - Other mainland outlets (Migu, BesTV, CCTV) also carry games; their terms are not public.
- **Tencent's own reach claims** (2019 NBA release): 490M fans watched on Tencent in 2018-19; 21M watched Finals Game 6.
- **Merchandise and licensing:** ESPN (2015) put China revenue from merchandise and licensing at more than $100M a year.
- **Owners' exposure:** ESPN reporting (story id 33938932) says NBA owners have more than $10B invested in China.
- **International rights overall** (secondary estimates):
  - Entertainment Strategy Guy, 7 Aug 2024, cites Forbes at about **$500M a year** from international media rights.
  - CNBC puts international at about **10%** of league revenue, including sponsorship.
  - If both hold, the 2020-25 Tencent deal (~$300M a year) would be **more than half** of all international media money. Flag that as an inference from two secondary estimates.
- **2019 Morey disruption:** already covered on 23 Oct (FHDAACSQ). Not repeated here.

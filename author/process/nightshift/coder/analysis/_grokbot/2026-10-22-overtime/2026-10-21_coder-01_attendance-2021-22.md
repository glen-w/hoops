# 2021-22 NBA regular-season attendance: official total

**Request:** 2026-10-21-coder-01 (med)
**Status:** done (best available figure; no NBA press release found)

## Answer
| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| **Total paid attendance** | **20,910,813** |
| Average per game | 17,186 |
| Capacity | 91.6% |
| Source | Sports Business Journal, Closing Bell, 13 Apr 2022, "NBA paid attendance": https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Daily/Closing-Bell/2022/04/13/NBA/ |
| Confidence | **MEDIUM-HIGH.** The figure is NBA paid attendance as reported by SBJ, which typically receives it from the league. I could not find an NBA press release stating a 2021-22 total. |

Context from the same SBJ item: all 30 teams hosted fans. Toronto played 14 home games with no fans or reduced capacity, and 11 games were rescheduled.

## Cross-checks
- **ESPN** team-home attendance summed over 30 teams: **20,862,460**. https://www.espn.com/nba/attendance/_/year/2022
- **Basketball-Reference** league average 695,475 per team × 30 = **≈20,864,257**. https://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_2022.html
- The ESPN and BRef sums are about 0.2% below SBJ. Team-level differences show up for BOS, ATL, OKC and SAC. The SBJ paid figure is the one to cite, with ESPN and BRef as corroboration.
- **pr.nba.com:** the 2021-22 "By the Numbers" infographic has no attendance figure. I viewed it.

# Verifying the 2020-21 attendance figure (RunRepeat "671k")

**Request:** 2026-10-21-coder-02 (med)
**Status:** done. Verdict: **671k is not supported. No official NBA total exists that I could find.**

## Verdict on 671k
- **Source:** RunRepeat, https://runrepeat.com/nba-attendance-statistics. It says "over 671 thousand" with an average of **893 per game**.
- **It is internally inconsistent.** 893 × 1,080 regular-season games ≈ **964k**, not 671k.
- **The same page has other obvious errors,** e.g. "219.9 million" for 2016-17 (the real figure was about 22.0M).
- **It doesn't match the team-level data.** The ESPN and BRef team sums are more than twice as large (below).
- **Recommendation:** drop 671k and the "96% drop" derived from it, in the CSV and in `brayton_chapter_outline.md`.

## Best available figure
| Source | 2020-21 total | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ESPN team-home sums | **1,492,605** | https://www.espn.com/nba/attendance/_/year/2021. Lists MIA, SAC, OKC and IND as 0, but MIA and IND did admit capped crowds for part of the season, so this is a **lower bound** |
| Basketball-Reference | **1,484,293** (26 teams) | https://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_2021.html. MIA, IND, SAC and OKC are blank; the league average of 49,476 is that total ÷ 30 |
| NBA official | **none found** | The league did not publish a season attendance total for 2020-21 |

**Suggested wording:** "roughly 1.5 million fans attended 2020-21 regular-season games (team-reported totals compiled by ESPN and Basketball-Reference; incomplete for several teams), against about 22 million in a normal season." That is a drop of about 93%, using 2018-19's 21.96M as the base.

**Confidence: LOW-MEDIUM.** The order of magnitude (about 1.5M) is solid. The exact figure isn't.

## Also flagged
- The CSV gives capacity at 25% for 2020-21. No source supports this; capacity limits varied by state and team.
- **2019-20 is not "zero attendance".** About 971 regular-season games were played in front of full crowds before the 11 Mar 2020 suspension, roughly 17.3M per BRef. Only the bubble had no fans. Fix `brayton_chapter_outline.md` and the CSV (see coder-03).

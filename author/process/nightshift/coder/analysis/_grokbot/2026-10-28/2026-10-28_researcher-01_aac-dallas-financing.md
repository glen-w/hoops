# researcher-01 — American Airlines Center (Dallas, 2001): financing structure

Night: 2026-10-28 (Grok Bot nightshift). Fetched 2026-10-28. Book: *Hoops*.
Confidence scale: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW / GAP. `[unverified]` = single secondary source or a search summary only.

## Bottom line

- **About a 30/70 public-private deal.** The arena cost about **$420M** (Ross Perot Jr., Tierra Grande 2008). The **City of Dallas paid a capped $125M** (~29.8%, my calc), funded by **$140.38M of 1998 special-tax revenue bonds** repaid from a new **2% hotel-occupancy tax + 5% short-term car-rental tax**, not the general fund or property tax. The **Arena Group** (Mavericks + Stars, through Center Operating Co. and Hillwood/Perot) paid the rest (~$295M) and **all overruns**.
- **The private side was mostly debt**, not equity. Per NSLI (2000) there were **~$215M of 25-year private-placement bonds** backed by arena revenues incl. naming rights. The Bank of New York was the arena's construction lender. Secondary source, MEDIUM-LOW.
- **No SEC filings exist.** The Mavericks, Stars, Center Operating Co. and Hillwood are all private. The city bonds were municipal (pre-EMMA, 1998) and were **fully repaid in Aug 2011** after aggressive optional calls from 2009, so the 2%/5% taxes ended.
- **The real "hidden" subsidy is the TIF + land/infrastructure.** The Sports Arena TIF (1998) repaid the developers about $25M of road, utility and remediation work (with interest; ~$38M all-in [MEDIUM]) and was extended in 2012. Victory Park development rode on it. Sroka (2017) argues "developer capture".
- Cost escalation: about **$230M (1998 plan) → $335M → ~$420M** [MEDIUM; the final figure is Perot's own "a $420 million building"].

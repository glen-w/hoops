## New findings
1. **Belo's stake is now sourced (HIGH).** Belo's FY1999 10-K says it bought **12.38% of the Mavericks LP plus a 6.19% non-voting interest in the arena-owning entity** for **~$24.5M**. Belo **sold both on 30 Jan 2002 for $27.0M** (pretax gain $2.375M, per the FY2001 10-K and Q1-2002 10-Q). This replaces yesterday's "$24M [unverified]". The 6.19% being exactly half of 12.38% suggests the Mavericks' side held about 50% of the arena entity and the Stars' side the other 50% [inference].
2. **AMR never disclosed the naming-rights deal (HIGH, absence).** EDGAR full-text search over AMR and American Airlines Inc for "naming rights" (2001+) returns 0 hits. The FY1998–FY2002 10-K405s mention AAC only through a subsidiary, **"American Airlines Center Foundation, Inc." (Texas)**, in Exhibit 21 for FY1999–FY2001. So the $195M / 30-year figure (and the $6.5M vs $5M a year conflict) cannot be settled from SEC filings. It was immaterial to AMR.
3. **The entity searches find nothing.** There is no EDGAR filer for **Center Operating Co.**, Dallas Mavericks, Radical Mavericks, Hicks Sports Group or Southwest Sports Group. The private placement (~$215M) was never registered, so **no SEC document gives the AAC's private debt terms**. Hicks Holdings Operating LLC (CIK 1775933) is a 2019+ filer and unrelated.
4. **Full-text search for "American Airlines Center" gives 183 hits (2001–2026)**, mostly noise (CMBS, proxies). Two useful side facts: Daktronics' 10-Ks (2002–03) cite "fully integrated" LED systems at AAC, and a 2020 SPAC S-1 bio says Andrew Tilliss "structured the financing for … American Airlines Center" [LOW; a lead on the private-placement arranger].
5. **Bond paydown detail (City of Dallas memo, 10 Dec 2010).** 1998A tax-exempt was paid in full by **16 Feb 2010**. 1998B taxable stood at $29.035M in Feb 2010 and $18.9M after the 2010 calls. $8.455M was redeemed on 15 Feb 2011, leaving $10.45M, with payoff targeted for end-2011, when the 2% hotel tax and 5% car-rental tax lapse. This matches yesterday's "paid off Aug 2011".
6. **EMMA.** The bonds' CUSIP-6 is **235375** ("Special Tax Revenue (Sports Arena Project) Bonds", per Dallas's BondLink page). The EMMA issuer page needs JS and terms acceptance, so its documents weren't pulled. The 1998 official statement predates EMMA's archive (2009), so the most EMMA could hold is the 2009–11 redemption notices. **Needs a manual click-through if wanted.**

# Financial sources found via paperful_ask

## Summary
Paperful_ask located **fragmented sources** covering different eras and categories:
- **1990s–2010**: Financial World (ceased), CBA definitions, historical studies (Price, Hausman)
- **2010–2023**: Santomier et al. (2023) with ticketing/Nike/merchandise snapshots
- **2023+**: No excerpt coverage for post-2024-25 revenue reporting

## Sources identified

### Revenue reporting and components
| Source | Coverage | What it has | What it lacks |
|--------|----------|-----------|---------------|
| CBA 1999 | 1999 | BRI definition (gate, media, novelties, concessions) | Current-year splits, revenue values |
| Price et al. (2010) | 1992-93 to 2007-08 | Gate revenue regression | Later seasons, category breakdown |
| Santomier et al. (2023) | 2010-11 to 2019-20 | Ticketing %, Nike, merchandise %, TNT/ESPN ~$2.6B/yr | Post-2020-21 seasons, sponsorship detail |
| Financial World | Pre-2000 | Team revenue (gate/media/stadium) breakdown | Ceased publication; archive access needed |
| Forbes | Ongoing | Annual team revenues (aggregate only) | Does not break down by category |

### Gate vs attendance
- **CBA**: "Regular season gate receipts" = official BRI component
- **Santomier**: Ticketing as % of total revenue (29.14% in 2010-11; 19.32% in 2019-20)
- **Unclear**: Whether "gate receipts" = attendance × avg ticket price, or separately reported

### Licensing and sponsorship
- **Nike**: $1B over 8 years (Santomier)
- **Merchandise**: >$1B annually (Santomier)
- **Missing**: Jersey-patch terms, arena-naming deal values, merchandise royalty rates, team-level sponsorship deals

## Gaps confirmed by paperful_ask

1. **Post-2024-25 national media terms**: No coverage. Needed: 2025-26+ partner split, package structure, term, streaming provisions.
2. **Local media post-2010**: RSN structure exists (Vrooman: FOX-19, Comcast-14, Rogers-7) but RSN consolidation/closure 2022-24 not in excerpts.
3. **Arena subsidies post-2010**: Only pre-2010 aggregate data (56.4% public share of $30B across 107 venues, all four leagues). Post-2010 NBA-specific cases missing.
4. **Direct-to-consumer / League Pass**: Neuhaus & Thomas describe ownership/financialization, but no subscriber, ARPU, or net revenue figures.
5. **Sponsorship inventory**: No contract register, jersey-patch terms, or arena-naming deal values in excerpts.

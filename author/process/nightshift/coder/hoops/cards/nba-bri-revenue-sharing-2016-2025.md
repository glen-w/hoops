# NBA Basketball-Related Income (BRI) and revenue sharing

Status: PENDING. No official season-by-season BRI or revenue-sharing table for 2016-17 through 2024-25 was found in the available derived and Paperful material. The closest grounded figures are nominal total revenue of $5,180M (2014-15), $5,866M (2015-16), $7,368M (2016-17), $8,007M (2017-18), and $8,759M (2018-19) from Rod Fort's Sports Business Pages/Forbes, which are not labeled BRI and stop at 2018-19. For 2018-19, BRI is described as only US$6.8 billion against reported total league revenue of US$8.8 billion, showing BRI excludes some revenue streams such as portions of luxury suite and arena naming-rights revenue. BRI generally includes ticketing, concessions, TV contracts, merchandising, luxury box sales, naming rights, and in-stadium revenues, while excluding team sales, expansion fees, facility construction, and Development League revenues. Revenue-sharing mechanics and season-level distributions remain unverified.

# NBA relocation and expansion fees (card)

Paperful returned one concrete Seattle figure and nothing usable for Sacramento. The 2008 SuperSonics departure is often described loosely as a relocation fee, but the sourced $45 million was a settlement paid to the City of Seattle to terminate the Key Arena lease, not a payment to the league. Howard Schultz sold the team and the Storm for $350 million to a group led by Clayton Bennett; the owners sought a $500 million Renton arena, were refused public money, and notified Commissioner David Stern in November 2007 of the intended move. Seattle sued; on 2 July 2008 the parties settled, the team left for Oklahoma City, and the city retained the SuperSonics name and history. The team began play as the Thunder in October 2008 (PENDING key; Paperful source 3EGEXSSS).

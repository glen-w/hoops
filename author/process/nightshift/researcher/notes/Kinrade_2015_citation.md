# Kinrade, Jackson & Ashford (2015)

**Full Citation:**
Kinrade, N.P., Jackson, R.C., & Ashford, K.J. (2015). Reinvestment, task complexity and decision making under pressure in basketball. *Psychology of Sport and Exercise*, 20, 11–19. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psychsport.2015.03.007

**Acquisition:**
- Source: Brunel University BURA repository (open access, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)
- PDF: https://bura.brunel.ac.uk/bitstream/2438/11345/1/Fulltext.pdf
- Also indexed at: Loughborough DSpace, NTU IREP

**Relevance to Ch03 Framework:**
Central to *decision under pressure* layer. Empirically grounds the reinvestment/rumination construct as predictor of choking under performance stress in basketball contexts. Task complexity modulates the effect—high-complexity decisions show greater reinvestment interference under pressure.

**Key Elements for Ch03:**
- **Reinvestment hypothesis:** Explicit monitoring of automatic processes under pressure degrades performance
- **DSRS subscale:** Rumination is the specific predictor (not overall reinvestment tendency)
- **Mechanism:** Pressure + high task complexity → rumination → slower, less accurate decisions
- **Basketball context:** Computer-based passing task (3-on-3 vs 5-on-5 complexity), parallels free-throw high-stakes scenarios

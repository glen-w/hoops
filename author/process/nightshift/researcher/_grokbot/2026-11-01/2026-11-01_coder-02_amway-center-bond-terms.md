## Debt service coverage
- **Pledge:** Orange County's Contract 6th-cent Tourist Development Tax (hotel tax), paid under the 2007 Community Venues Interlocal Agreement. HIGH
- **Bonds outstanding at 30 Sep 2024:** 2017A senior $159.87M (S&P A / Fitch A, AGM-insured, final maturity 2038) and 2017B second lien $22.415M. Total $182.285M. HIGH
- **Reserves:** senior liquidity reserve $7.702M plus senior DSR $7.702M. Each second-lien reserve is $1.081M. HIGH
- **Revenues and debt service:** FY2024 contract revenues were $29,955,271. Bond-year 2025 debt service is $17,561,750. My own calculation gives coverage of about **1.71x aggregate and 1.94x senior**. The figures mix fiscal and bond years, so this is MEDIUM.
- **Stress history:** the 2008C DSR was drawn in 2013 ($1.23M) and 2014 ($0.49M), and the 2017B DSR was drawn in Nov 2021 ($0.47M). Ratings went from 2010 lows of CCC/CC on the 2008B/C bonds to Fitch A in Mar 2024. HIGH

## Public subsidy share
- The project cost $480M ($380M construction, $40M land/site, $60M parking/roads).
- The Magic put in about $50M directly plus about $12M in present value of use payments, roughly 13%. That puts the public share at about **87%**. LOW: this comes from a parse of a third-party mirror of the county sources-and-uses sheet.

## Lease / reserve terms
- The use agreement runs 25 years at a $1M/yr use fee (Marquette lease summary §5.1). The City has exclusive maintenance responsibility (§7.1). **No team-funded capital reserve found.**
- The Nov 2024 "Kia Interlocal" commits $226M of county TDT to capital improvements, with new bonds expected in 2027.
- The 2016 State Sales Tax bonds ($20.36M) freed more than $5M for capital improvements.

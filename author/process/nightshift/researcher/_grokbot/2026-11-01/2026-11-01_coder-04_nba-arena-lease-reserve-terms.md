# Post-2010 NBA arena lease reserve / capital repair terms (coder-04)
Retrieved 2026-11-01. Data: `2026-11-01_coder-04_nba-arena-lease-reserve-terms.csv` / `.parquet` (13 rows).

| Arena | Term | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Little Caesars Arena (2017) | Repair reserve of $500k/yr, +4%/yr from 2018, split 50/50 between the DDA and Olympia; sits 13th in the bond waterfall (2018 OS p.9) | HIGH |
| Footprint Center (2019 deal) | Capital reserve: City $2M/yr plus Suns $1M/yr (Cronkite News 2019; Zotero 5CDIBM4W) | MEDIUM |
| Fiserv Forum (2018) | ArenaCo pays all maintenance, repair, replacement and renovation costs plus $1M/yr rent escalating (lease §§4.1, 5.1, 5.3 via Marquette summary). About $60M of cumulative team capital-reserve deposits [summary-only; LFB paper unreachable] | HIGH / LOW |
| Golden 1 Center (2016) | 2013 term sheet: $1/ticket Capital Repair Fee into a Capital Repairs Reserve Fund; Kings responsible for all capital repairs. Lease rent $6.5M/yr escalating 3–5% (2016 Treasurer report). Bond DSR $18.38M | MEDIUM / HIGH |
| Spectrum Center | City contractually obliged to make capital repairs and NBA-required upgrades; $110M COP BAN in 2023 (NC LGC) | HIGH |
| State Farm Arena (2018 reno) | $3/ticket facility charge covers ongoing capital repairs and maintenance (Atlanta Council, 2017) | HIGH |
| Amway/Kia Center (2010) | City maintains (use agreement §7.1); 2024 county TDT commitment of $226M for capital improvements | MEDIUM / HIGH |
| Frost Bank Center | Spurs pay $1.3M/yr license, usable for debt service (to 2032); O&M handled by MOU, with no county O&M contribution | HIGH |

**Pattern:** reserves are funded either by per-ticket fees (Sacramento $1, Atlanta $3), by fixed annual deposits (Detroit $500k +4%, Phoenix $3M/yr combined), or by putting all capital responsibility on the team (Milwaukee, Sacramento). Orlando and Charlotte leave capital repair with the city.

## Debt service coverage
- The bonds are special limited obligations secured by pledged tax-increment revenues, a Senior Lien DSRF and a Revenue Stabilization Fund that is fed from excess Catalyst revenues (OS p.21, p.27). HIGH
- The projected coverage in the consultant's Scenario A is 4.54x in FY2019, 2.55x in FY2020 and 2.16x in FY2021, then about 2.07–2.39x until it reaches 2.15x in FY2048 (OS p.47). These are **projections, not actuals**. MEDIUM
- The DSRF deposit from 2018A is $21,231,000. The requirement is the standard three-prong test (10% of principal, MADS, or 125% of average annual debt service) (OS p.7, p.21). HIGH
- The Series 2024 refunding (about $200M) is rated BBB+ by S&P, which cites MADS coverage of about 4.5x. KBRA rates it A/stable at 4.25x pro-forma MADS for FY2023. [summary-only] MEDIUM

## Public subsidy share
- Crain's (23 May 2017) puts the public share at 37.6%: $324.1M public out of a $862.9M total (Zotero ASQSSF2Q, reused). MEDIUM
- The 2013 announcement split a $650M estimate 56% Olympia and 44% public. MEDIUM
- The arena is owned by the DDA and is not taxed. Under the MDA, Olympia must make at least $200M of ancillary investment, and the DDA reimburses up to $74M from excess Catalyst revenues (OS pp.10–11). HIGH

## Lease / repair reserve terms
- Under Concession Agreement §12.6, the repair reserve receives **$500,000 per year, rising 4% a year from 2018, shared equally** between the DDA and Olympia Entertainment Events Center LLC (OS p.9; definition at App. C-33).
- The Repair Fund sits **THIRTEENTH** in the Catalyst revenue waterfall. The issuer covenants not to make adverse amendments to the Repair Amount. HIGH

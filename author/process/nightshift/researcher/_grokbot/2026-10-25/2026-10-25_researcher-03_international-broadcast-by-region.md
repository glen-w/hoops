# researcher-03: International broadcast partners and rates by region
Fetch date for all sources: 2026-10-25. The data file is `2026-10-25_researcher-03_intl_broadcast_rates.csv` (22 rows). Its columns are region, market, partner, years, reported_value, source_url, fetch_date, confidence, note. I added `market` and `note` to the requested columns.

## Bottom line
**Partners are well documented. Values almost never are.** Outside China, I found reported per-market values for only two markets:
- **Japan (Rakuten 2017):** US$225M over 5 years, about ¥25.4B (Sanspo).
- **India:** Sony's 5-year deal at about **$20-21M**, then Viacom18's 3-year deal from 2021-22 at about **$1.5M** (exchange4media, "industry sources"). That is roughly a 93% fall. It is worth a line in the book: Indian demand did not hold up the price.

**The structural story is Amazon.** From 2025-26, Prime Video holds a global NBA game package everywhere except Greater China, Poland, the Nordics and the Netherlands, with Canada from 2026-27. It holds an **expanded package** in the UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Brazil and Mexico.

Local broadcasters now sit beside Prime rather than holding exclusive rights:
- Sky in the UK, Germany and Italy
- beIN in France
- DAZN in Spain
- ESPN/Disney+ in Brazil and Mexico

No per-market split of Amazon's ~$1.8B a year (press-reported) is public.

## Caveats
- "Not disclosed" means that **no public figure was found**. It does not mean there is no fee.
- SportBusiness and SBJ may carry estimates behind their paywalls. They were not accessed.
- Several rows rely on search results whose URLs I didn't keep. Those are marked medium or low confidence in the CSV.

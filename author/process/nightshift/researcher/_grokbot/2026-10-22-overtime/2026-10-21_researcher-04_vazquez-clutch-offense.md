## Core argument
Close endgames ("clutch") decide many results. Systematic observation can identify which offensive patterns succeed when the score margin is one possession.

## Methods
- Observational methodology. The sample was **709 offensive actions from 117 EuroLeague games (2022/23)**, covering the final 60 s of the fourth quarter and overtime with a margin of 3 points or fewer.
- Ad hoc observation instrument coded in LINCE PLUS, with reliability checks.
- Analysis: chi-square and association tests, plus **T-pattern detection** (THEME 6).

## Key findings
- Teams **winning** at the start of the play scored more effectively (62.8%) than teams that were tied (37.7%) or losing (44.9%), largely by drawing fouls and free throws.
- Home and away success was almost identical (50.9% vs 49.3%).
- The more effective patterns had few or no passes, were finished by point guards or centres, and came in transition or fast breaks.
- The T-patterns suggest coaches should design short, direct actions for clutch possessions.

# Why Basketball is Good

Basketball's cultural resonance stems from paradoxical accessibility fused with boundless complexity. Yet basketball's deepest appeal resides in its role as site of human striving: individual skill, collective choreography, and contingency intersect meaningfully. A playground game that scales to professional spectacle, basketball mirrors fundamental questions about access, excellence, and belonging in stratified societies.

## Books & Gatekeeping in Basketball Media

Paperful_ask located critical frameworks on NBA gatekeeping but few dedicated book-length narratives on labour accountability.

**Sourced:**

**Leonard (2012)** — NBA uses crises to discipline players; develops "assault on blackness" framework for understanding franchise control narratives and gatekeeping.

**Araton (2005)** — Critical examination of NBA culture; critiques franchise construction of player narratives; addresses player agency disputes within control structures.

**Fortunato (2001)** *The Ultimate Assist* — Documents NBA–broadcast gatekeeping; shows how franchise–network partnerships shape narrative control.

**Jozsa (2018)** *Sports Capitalism* — Maps franchise revenue streams and cost structures; reveals financial incentives underlying labour decisions.

These works approach gatekeeping through player critique, media strategy, and business analysis, but do not form a dedicated archive of book-length investigations into franchise labour disputes, journalist access restrictions, or ownership accountability.

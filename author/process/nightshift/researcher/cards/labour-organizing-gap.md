# Labour Organizing in NBA Arenas: Missing Accounts

Labour organizing in NBA arenas remains undocumented in book form. Paperful search returned sources on ownership demographics (Brooks & McKail 2008), league gatekeeping and athlete activism (Maharaj 1997, Wulf 2019), and arena development (Downs & Seifried 2023). None address worker campaigns against vendors (Aramark, Levy) or franchise labour-dispute decisions.

The gap is structural: analyses of how NBA ownership manages labour exist, but accounts of worker organizing and vendor consolidation are absent. Labour-organizing sources may exist under different keywords (contingent labour, food-service operations, union campaigns) or outside the current Zotero mirror. The absence suggests either: (1) labour organizing in arenas is undocumented in books (confined to journalism and advocacy org reports); (2) such sources are catalogued differently; or (3) labour organizing campaigns are recent or localized, lacking book-length treatment.

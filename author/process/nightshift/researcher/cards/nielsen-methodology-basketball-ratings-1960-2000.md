# Nielsen Methodology & Basketball Ratings 1960-2000

{Kanazawa & Funk, 2001; QF6H4KE9} Nielsen local-programming ratings derived from household samples within ratings areas; viewing patterns compiled from electronic meters (channel/channel-time) plus demographic diaries kept by household members for selected periods. Data acknowledged as not "perfectly" reflecting larger population viewing habits. {Marshall, 2018; L3F8BYYA} People-meter introduced 1987 via computing advances, replacing household-only metrics with individual-level measurement. {Kanazawa & Funk, 2001; QF6H4KE9} Nielsen Media Research compiled "Sport Facts: Local Market Monthly Sports Reports" (e.g., January 1997) containing advertising air-time and expenditure data for specific games — a market-data product, not a methodological report.

# Arena construction cost evolution: ENR CCI 2000–2026

NBA arena construction and renovation financing from 2000 to 2026 requires adjustment for construction-specific cost inflation. The Engineering News-Record Construction Cost Index (ENR CCI, 20-city composite, 1913=100) rose from 6,221 in 2000 to 11,597 in 2026—an 86.4% nominal increase. Year-over-year growth peaked during 2004–2008 (averaging 7.8% annually, pre-crisis expansion), contracted sharply in 2008–2009 (−1.4% to 0.4%), recovered through 2018, and accelerated post-2020 (9.1% in 2021). This construction-specific deflator diverges from general CPI inflation, reflecting labor scarcity and materials volatility. Researchers examining arena financing through bond prospectuses, franchise capex decisions, and venue modernization must use the ENR CCI rather than general inflation to compare nominal debt service, refunding bond par values, and construction contract amounts across decades. The deflator reveals cost regimes during major arena openings and renovations, informing both public finance risk assessment and private franchise investment strategy.

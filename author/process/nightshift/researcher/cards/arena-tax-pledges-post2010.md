# Post-2010 arena bonds and dedicated hotel or car-rental taxes

No source retrieved identifies a post-2010 NBA arena whose construction bonds were repaid from a dedicated hotel or car-rental tax, nor names the bond indenture or tax-allocation agreement recording such a pledge.

The nearest evidence is pre-2010 and broader than the NBA. {Irizarry, 2017} notes that Harris County, Houston financed baseball, football and basketball stadiums with motel/hotel and rental-car taxes, with expected revenues falling roughly 12 per cent in 2009. The same source describes Orlando's Amway Center as backed by an increase in the tourism development tax, with bonds issued around 2008 and a downgrade in 2010.

{Swindell, 1998} states generally that cities and counties use special taxes on alcohol, tobacco, hotel rooms and car rentals to build or operate facilities. {Humphreys and Nowak, 2017} list recent TIF/CRL-financed facilities — Rogers Place, KFC Yum! Center, a proposed Chicago arena and a Milwaukee arena — but the mechanism is incremental property-tax revenue, not a dedicated hotel or car-rental levy. {Chipperfield, 2010} reports a payoff schedule for 1998 arena bonds without specifying the pledged tax source.

Treat the dedicated-tax route as unverified pending official statements.

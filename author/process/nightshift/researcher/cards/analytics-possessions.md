# Analytics: Core Analytical Frameworks

Modern basketball analytics rests upon the **possession concept**, a unifying framework that normalises statistics to a common unit per 100 possessions or per minute for fair comparison (Kubatko, 2007). This foundational approach underpins all advanced metrics.

Player evaluation relies on **Player Efficiency Rating (PER)**, which aggregates positive and negative player actions into a per-minute rating capturing scoring efficiency, assists, rebounds and defensive contributions (Gutiérrez, 2024). **True Shooting Percentage (TS%)** and **Effective Field Goal Percentage (eFG%)** refine offensive assessment by accounting for shot selection and efficiency.

For broader impact, **Value Over Replacement Player (VORP)** estimates points per 100 possessions contributed above replacement-level, serving as a key analytic for team-building and roster construction. Modern scholarship increasingly integrates deep learning and tracking data to extend box-score metrics beyond historical limitations (Terner & Franks, 2024).

**Culinary Partnerships and Local Cuisine in NBA Arena Dining (1990–2026)**

As NBA franchises have matured as lifestyle brands, stadium dining has evolved beyond utilitarian concessions toward curated culinary experiences. Recent decades see franchises partnering with regional and celebrity chefs, integrating local cuisine into arena menus, and positioning food service as an extension of franchise identity and community connection. The Brooklyn Nets' incorporation of local Brooklyn vendors at Barclays Center, the New Orleans Pelicans' emphasis on Creole and Cajun cuisine, and celebrity-chef collaborations across franchises signal a shift toward food as cultural ambassador. Yet scholarship documenting this culinary-franchise integration—examining how food partnerships affect fan experience, community perception, labour conditions in back-of-house operations, and local supplier relationships—remains thin.

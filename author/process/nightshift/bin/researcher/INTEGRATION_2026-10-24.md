**FIN** (2,300+ w grounded)
- Two strategies post-2010: private (Chase $1.4B, Intuit ~$1.8–2B) vs. public (Golden 1/Fiserv/Little Caesars 38–48% public, Barclays PILOT bonds).
- Economics consensus: 130+ studies, no net growth, so subsidy is rarely justified; true public cost underestimated by ~40%.
- Arena neighborhood effects: property values rise (Keeler, Chikish, Propheter), but no causal displacement study; Edmonton qualitative evidence (Scherer et al.) shows gentrification mechanism.
- Counterintuitive: team departure (Sonics, Bobcats) *raised* nearby prices—teams were disamenities.
- Gaps: causal displacement, maintenance/renovation costs, tax abatement renewal, intl comparators (EU/AU), subleasing, Intuit Dome outcomes.
- Source: hoops/FIN_grounded_2026-10-23.md

**STADIUM** (1,800+ w grounded)
- Home-court advantage real (~58–60%), mediated by player behavior/adrenaline (rebounding, intensity), not ref bias (Ganz, Gong, Szabó, Leota, Ehrlich & Potter).
- COVID evidence: empty arenas erased ~2.1 pt home edge; small crowds kept most; refs not biased.
- Arena as anchor: property values rise (Golden 1, Barclays), but retail survival falls, police response slower on game nights; no building permit surge.
- Qualitative evidence (Edmonton) shows gentrification impact; causal displacement not measured.
- Gaps: technical fouls/refs mechanism, concessions economics, disability access, heat/air/climate effects, intl arena ops, historic closures.
- Source: hoops/STADIUM_grounded_2026-10-23.md

# Sonkeng (2020) — Pickup Basketball as Self-Care

**Source:** `hoops/Sonkeng - 2020 - Exploring the healing touch of pickup basketball as a self-care method for educators and -- 24ECN7VH`

## Summary
Ethnographic study (U Georgia Kinesiology) on pickup basketball's "healing touch" for educators & helping professionals. Qualitative: participant observation + interviews.

## Key Themes
- **Affirmative/empowering space** — validation for emotionally drained workers
- **Unique intimacy** — small court, no pads, frequent high-fives/hugs/handshakes → positive physical contact
- **Transcends boundaries** — race, gender, class, culture; "universal language"
- **Accessible/affordable** — minimal gear, free courts vs. formal therapy

## Recommendations
- Skill-tiered runs to reduce intimidation (women, older adults)
- Helping pros should join local runs
- Social media awareness campaigns

## Chapter Seeds
- *The Court as Clinic* — pickup as informal mental-health infrastructure
- *Touch in a Touch-Starved World* — basketball's unique skin-contact density
- *Open Run, Open Heart* — how anonymity + intimacy coexist

# Key Factors in Basketball

The excerpts identify several clusters of key factors that influence basketball performance and game outcomes:

## Performance / Statistical Factors

- **Shooting efficiency** (including free-throw percentage) is repeatedly highlighted as one of the most significant determinants of game outcomes; in the WNBA it accounts for roughly 42 % of the impact on results [S1].
- **Rebounds** (especially defensive rebounds) and **assists** are consistently listed among the most frequently cited key performance indicators [S1].
- **Three-point shooting** has emerged as a key tactical element of modern basketball, reshaping offensive strategies; higher three-point accuracy is consistently linked to team success [S1].
- Tománek et al. frame their analysis around **Oliver's Four Factors**: effective field-goal percentage (eFG%), turnover percentage (TOV%), offensive rebound percentage (ORB%), and free-throw rate (FTR), and test how well these predict winning outcomes in women's basketball [S1].
- In 3×3 basketball, factors that raise offensive productivity while lowering defensive concession rates are identified as key success drivers, though their numerical weight varies with team strategy and opponent quality [S6].

## Tactical and Technical Factors

- **Offensive and defensive techniques** specific to each position (guard, forward, center) are identified as key determinants of scoring, with differences between men's and women's players at the Olympic level [S3].
- **Tactical methods, coaching decisions, and game data** are listed alongside individual player attributes as factors influencing team performance [S5].
- Regional playing-style differences matter: European championships feature more organized offense and a slower pace, while African teams show more free throws and turnovers [S1].

## Physical and Psychological Factors

- **Body size / height** is described as a major factor that has shaped the game since its inception; the premium on height and rebounding has been a recurring theme [S2].
- The **dynamic interplay between physical, technical, and psychological factors** is called out as an area needing further investigation for a comprehensive understanding of competitive performance [S5].
- **Psychological aspects** are explicitly named among the key determinants of success [S5].

## Technological / Analytical Factors

- **Artificial intelligence** (machine learning, deep neural networks, reinforcement learning) is identified as a key factor in basketball analysis and strategy development [S4].

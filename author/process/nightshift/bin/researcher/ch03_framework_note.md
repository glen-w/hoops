# Ch03 Framework: Decision Under Pressure (Cognitive Layers)

**Scope:** Integrate three interdependent cognitive factors affecting free-throw performance:

1. **Decision-Making Under Pressure (Kinrade 2015)**
   - Reinvestment hypothesis: explicit monitoring → interference
   - Rumination as key DSRS subscale
   - Task complexity moderates effect
   - Prediction: High-pressure + complex shots → ruminative processing → degraded kinematics + accuracy

2. **Fatigue + Kinematic Shift (Rusdiana 2020)**
   - Fatigue alters free-throw mechanics (ankle/knee/hip angles)
   - Compensatory movement patterns emerge
   - Compound effect: fatigue narrows motor flexibility available for pressure-induced corrections

3. **Workload + Sustainability (Hore 2018)**
   - ML model: player workload predicts injury/performance decline
   - Chronic fatigue modulates acute pressure response
   - Prediction: High workload → diminished cognitive + motor reserves under pressure

**Interaction Model:**
High workload → basal fatigue → reduced motor margin → pressure applied → rumination amplified → kinematic breakdown → missed free throw.

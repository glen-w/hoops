# Hoops: An Uncommon Field Guide to the Game of Basketball

Basketball is rarely studied for what it truly is: a space of healing, labor, and measurable human excellence. This guide examines three uncommon angles through peer-reviewed research:

1. **Wellness Through Play**: How pickup basketball serves as accessible self-care and healing, particularly for educators and trauma survivors.
2. **Performance Indices**: How modern analytics measure the full scope of player contribution—beyond scoring.
3. **Labor Politics of Injury**: How the medical framing of basketball injury masks deeper questions about player protection and workplace safety.

Each chapter grounds its argument in empirical study, refusing both romanticization and reduction to spectacle.

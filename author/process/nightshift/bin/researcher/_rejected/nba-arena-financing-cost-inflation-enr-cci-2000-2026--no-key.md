# NBA Arena Financing: Cost Inflation Adjustment via ENR CCI (2000–2026)

## Data and Method

The Engineering News-Record Construction Cost Index (ENR CCI; 20-city, 1913=100) is a professional standard for construction cost escalation. Using ENR CCI annual values 2000–2026 (retrieved November 2026, from municipal bond prospectuses and public databases), nominal arena financing figures can be converted to constant 2026 dollars.

## Application to Arena Bonds

Little Caesars Arena (Detroit) 2018 debt: nominal $287.4M issuance. Amway Center (Orlando) 2017 bonds: nominal $159.9M outstanding. Deflating nominal debt service schedules by ENR CCI year-over-year changes reveals real cost burden on public authorities and isolates franchise debt assumptions embedded in prospectuses from construction-cost inflation.

## Why This Matters for FIN

Franchise profitability claims and public subsidy calculations require constant-dollar comparison. ENR CCI enables direct comparison across decades and arenas, isolating construction-cost inflation from league-wide revenue growth or competitive imbalance drivers.

# Chapter 3: Cognitive Mechanics Under Pressure
## Synthesis: Reinvestment, Fatigue, and Workload as Unified Model

### The Three-Source Framework

Basketball free throws expose a cognitive mechanism common across all high-pressure tasks: **reinvestment collapse under fatigue in high-workload states**.

**Kinrade (2015)** identified reinvestment as conscious, rule-based override of automaticity under pressure—the shooter shifts from subconscious execution to deliberate control. This reallocation of attentional resources toward explicit technique monitoring reveals the threat response: when stakes rise, the skilled executor moves backward, destabilizing the very automaticity that enabled skill.

**Rusdiana (2020)** documented the kinematic consequences: fatigue degrades the coordinated sequence that supports free-throw accuracy. Central fatigue (neural) and peripheral fatigue (muscular) both corrupt the reproducible arm trajectory and release timing that accuracy demands. Crucially, fatigue amplifies noise in motor execution—the micro-adjustments that normally occur unconsciously become unreliable under neural depletion.

**Hore (2018)** examined sustainability across seasons: NBA workload (games played, minutes, back-to-backs) predicts performance decline and injury risk. High cumulative load erodes the baseline from which acute fatigue operates; a worn athlete starts each game already depleted, compounding the fatigue-kinematic sensitivity.

### The Interaction: Reinvestment × Fatigue × Workload

The synthesis reveals a **cascade**, not independent effects:

1. **Workload sets baseline depletion**: High-load seasons (heavy minutes, frequent games) elevate chronic fatigue. The athlete enters each game with depleted neural reserves. This is not conscious pressure; it is physiological ceiling reduction.

2. **Acute pressure triggers reinvestment**: The high-stakes free throw (close game, playoff moment) shifts attention toward conscious technique—precisely when neural resources are already constrained.

3. **Reinvestment × fatigue = motor collapse**: The shift to explicit control requires intact inhibitory and working-memory capacity. Under central fatigue, these executive functions degrade. The conscious override intended to stabilize performance instead adds noise: the athlete must simultaneously fight fatigue-induced drift in baseline motor control while implementing deliberate technique cues.

4. **Kinematic destabilization**: The result is observable: release point drifts, arm angle varies, follow-through becomes inconsistent. These are not random; they reflect the failure of both the automatic system (fatigued) and the conscious override (resource-starved).

### Cognitive Mechanisms: Pressure-Induced Rumination → Kinematic Degradation → Performance Failure

**Rumination phase** (Kinrade's reinvestment):
- Pressure activates threat detection (high stakes)
- Threat shifts attention inward: "How am I executing this?"
- Conscious rule-based monitoring begins
- Problem: this overrides the automatic sequence that does not require conscious scrutiny

**Kinematic phase** (Rusdiana's fatigue sensitivity):
- Under fatigue, the automatic system is already compromised (central neural depletion affects timing, proprioceptive integration)
- Conscious override attempts to compensate but cannot fully replace the nuanced, parallel processing of automaticity
- The shooter now depends on explicit limb-positioning cues while the neural substrate executing them is degraded
- Motor output becomes noisier: small variations in pre-movement neural state lead to larger kinematic variance

**Failure phase** (observable collapse):
- Increased release-point variability → miss more off-target
- Timing degradation → arc inconsistency, reduced arch → increased miss rate
- Confidence feedback loop: missed attempts under pressure reinforce threat perception, deepening rumination on next attempt

**Workload modulation** (Hore's finding):
- High-load athletes begin with reduced neural reserve
- Reinvestment-under-fatigue becomes more likely and more severe (less spare capacity to recover from executive demand)
- Season-long sustainability erodes: as cumulative fatigue deepens across the season, the threshold for pressure-triggered reinvestment lowers

### Integration: A Testable Model

Free-throw accuracy under pressure should correlate with:
- **Pressure effect size**: Proportional to (acute load / available reserve)
- **Fatigue sensitivity**: Amplified by high seasonal workload
- **Reinvestment susceptibility**: More pronounced in players with lower automaticity baseline or higher explicit-processing reliance
- **Kinematic signal**: Increased shot variability visible in release point, arc, and follow-through under pressure conditions, especially for high-load athletes

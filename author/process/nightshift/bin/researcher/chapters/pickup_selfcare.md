# Pickup Basketball as Self-Care

Grounded in: `hoops/Sonkeng - 2020 - Exploring the healing touch of pickup basketball as a self-care method for educators and -- 24ECN7VH`

## Core Argument
Pickup basketball functions as an accessible, embodied self-care practice for educators and helping professionals. Its therapeutic power stems from **positive physical touch** (high-fives, handshakes, hugs) in a context of close proximity and minimal protective gear — a rare "healing touch" in modern professional life.

## Key Themes
- **Affirmation & empowerment**: Participants describe the court as a space of validation, especially for those in emotionally draining roles.
- **Intimacy across boundaries**: Touch transcends race, gender, class, culture — pickup as "universal language."
- **Low barrier**: Minimal equipment, free public courts, no appointment needed.
- **Skill-level gaps**: Intimidation deters women and older adults; authors recommend designated beginner-friendly runs.

## Chapter Beats
1. The anthropology of the high-five: why skin contact matters.
2. The court as third place for helping professionals.
3. Designing inclusive pickup: skill tiers, mixed-gender norms, community stewardship.
4. Prescription: "Two hours of pickup" as legitimate clinical self-care.

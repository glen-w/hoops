# Michael Jordan: The Sure Thing

Grounded in: `hoops/Unknown - 2024 - Michael Jordan, In General -- 29U4MCE6` (R.B. Glaser, *Columbia: A Journal of Literature and Art*, 2006)

## Core Argument
Jordan is not a person but a **cultural fixed point** — "23, 23, 45, 23" — around which American memory, nostalgia, and identity organize. The essay separates the *icon* (dunk, pause, earring, encoded in montage) from the *human* (middle name, fear of swimming, marriage, mortality).

## Key Themes
- **Heroless origins**: Basketball began spontaneous, nameless; evolved through ABA/BAA/SBA/NBA into global spectacle.
- **Media as embalming**: ESPN Classic, bedroom posters, video loops ensure "Jordan keeps shooting in an empty court."
- **Associative method**: Stream-of-consciousness links Jordan to Salinger, Dick Dale, Mickey Mouse, LaGuardia smashing pinball machines — basketball as American dreamtime.
- **Icon vs. human**: The icon is timeless, constructed; the human is finite, scared, dying.

## Chapter Beats
1. The empty court: media repetition as secular liturgy.
2. Before the icon: basketball's heroless birth.
3. The number as totem: 23 → 45 → 23.
4. What the icon hides: fear of water, middle name, eventual death.
5. Why we need the Sure Thing: cultural anchor in flux.

## A. Free throws under pressure
- hoops/Arthur-Cameselle - 2024 - Effects of Brief Mindfulness Training on Basketball Free-Throw Shooting Performance Under -- SN6FE6IH
  - Crossref: Arthur-Cameselle, J. N., & Keeler, L. A. Effects of Brief Mindfulness Training on Basketball Free-Throw Shooting Performance Under Pressure: A Randomized Controlled Trial of Dosage Response. *Journal of Clinical Sport Psychology, 19*(4), 357-374. https://doi.org/10.1123/jcsp.2023-0063
  - Year mismatch: the Zotero filename says 2024 and the Crossref issue says 2025. Not resolved.
  - Abstract (paraphrased by the fetch tool, not quoted): 46 basketball players heard either a 6-min or 15-min mindfulness audio or a control audio, then shot under high pressure. Groups did not differ in anxiety, mindfulness or average shooting. Only the control group shot worse on the second high-pressure attempt; the authors suggest this may reflect a protective effect of the training.
  - Ch03 role: an intervention RCT with a mostly null result. It is **not** mechanism evidence and says nothing about reinvestment, the wrist kinematics or prefrontal override. Use it only in a "what helps" aside, hedged.
  - Precursor (not in local list, so requested): Wolch, N. J., Arthur-Cameselle, J. N., Keeler, L. A., & Suprak, D. N. (2021). The effects of a brief mindfulness intervention on basketball free-throw shooting performance under pressure. *Journal of Applied Sport Psychology, 33*(5), 510-526. https://doi.org/10.1080/10413200.2020.1720044
  - Lead (Crossref only, venue = PsycEXTRA dataset): Chen, J., Chen, R., & Tang, X. (2023). Effects of brief mindfulness training on quiet eye in basketball free throw under pressure. https://doi.org/10.1037/e503872025-001

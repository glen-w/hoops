# Free-throw kinematics under pressure: research gap & next steps

## Outcome evidence (grounded)
- **Cao et al. 2011; Toma 2017; shufinskiy 2026-10-22 analysis:**
  - Late-close (final minute, ≤3 pt margin): −2.5 pp (trailing: −5.3 pp)
  - Playoff ambient pressure: −0.8 pp
  - NO workload×pressure interaction
  - **Mechanism:** Unknown at outcome level. Pressure reduces accuracy but not through fatigue amplification.

## Kinematics mechanisms (partial evidence)
**Mullineaux finding:** Late variability in release marks misses. Late release timing error ⇒ miss.
**ASU 2017 thesis (Orn):** Under pressure, shooters show more "novice-like" movement patterns; significant pressure × movement-restriction interactions in elbow-knee correlations.
**Basketball kinematic pattern:** Expert automaticity (motor program integrity) vs. explicit control under stress.

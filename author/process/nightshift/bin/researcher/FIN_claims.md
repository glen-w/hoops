## NBA Salary Cap and Luxury Tax Structure

Under the 2013 CBA, the salary cap is set at fifty-one percent (51%) of projected Basketball-Related Income, distributed equally across thirty teams. The luxury tax threshold ("Tax Level") is fixed at sixty-one percent (61%) of an equal share of BRI; exceeding teams face financial penalties. The 1999 CBA established the cap at 48.04% of BRI. Players collectively maintain compensation between 49–51% of league revenue, with guaranteed minimums of 57% including benefits and international rights fees.

# Hoops: Uncommon Field Guide

## Court
- Geometry: 94ft x 50ft (NBA), key, three-point arc (23.9ft NBA, 22ft college)
- Surface: Hardwood (maple), indoor climate control
- Markings: Baseline, sideline, free-throw line, center circle

## Rules
- 4 quarters (12 min NBA, 10 min FIBA), shot clock 24s (NBA), 8s half-court
- Fouls: 5/team (NBA), bonus free throws after 5
- Travel: Pivot foot violation, double dribble
- Violations: Backcourt, 3-second lane

## Culture
- Global: NBA international games, FIBA World Cup
- Community: Pickup games, AAU youth leagues
- Fashion: Jersey culture, sneaker endorsements
- Rivalries: Lakers-Celtics, Knicks-Heat

## Data
- Stats: PER, BPM, True Shooting %
- Tracking: Player movement (Second Spectrum), shot charts
- Analytics: Pace, eFG%, defensive rating
- Sources: ESPN PBP data (23VJDADZ), sportsdataverse

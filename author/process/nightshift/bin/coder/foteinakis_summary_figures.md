## Sample
- 14,688 games: 12,150 NBA regular season and 2,538 EuroLeague (regular season plus top-16).
- 12,150 + 2,538 = 14,688, which matches.
- Nine seasons between 2012-13 and 2022-23. 2019-20 was excluded for the pandemic.
- Data: stats.nba.com and euroleague.net.
- Method: Shapiro-Wilk (all variables non-normal), Mann-Whitney U, p < 0.05, JASP v0.19.

## Trends reported
- The NBA's 3-point frequency grew faster and nearly closed the gap in recent seasons.
- Both leagues trended up in shooting efficiency. The NBA's recent eFG% rise was steeper.
- Both leagues saw 2PA per 100 possessions decline.
- Authors attribute differences to court dimensions, defensive rules and tactical philosophy.

## 1. Television Rights

### 2016–2024 Media Deal
Nine-year agreement (2016-17 to 2024-25) with ABC/ESPN and Turner Broadcasting (TNT). Total: **~$24 billion** over nine years, or **~$2.6 billion annually**. Partners split [source: Sports Illustrated per league-income-sources.md].

Official NBA PR (2014-10-06) confirms partners and term; dollar amounts are secondary sources (MEDIUM confidence). Per-team allocation averages to approximately **$103 million per team per year** (2024-25).

### 2025–2035 Media Deal
Eleven-year agreement (2025-26 to 2035-36) with Disney (ABC/ESPN), NBCUniversal (NBC/Peacock), and Amazon Prime Video. Total: **~$77 billion** over eleven years, or **~$6.9 billion annually**. Official NBA PR (2024) confirms partners and term; dollar amounts from CNBC (citing people familiar), not audited league release (MEDIUM confidence). Per-team split estimated **~$143 million per year** for 2025-26.

**Data gap:** Official dollar amounts not published by NBA. Comparison to other sports requires secondary-source alignment.

## 3. Attendance

Regular-season attendance surpassed **22.3 million** in 2024-25 (exact total not yet published). 2022-23 and 2023-24 data available from league-income-ingredients.csv.

- 2022-23: comparable period; full dataset in sources.

**Data gap:** No ticket revenue, suite revenue, or local arena gate allocation. Attendance is a volume proxy, not a revenue measure.

## 4. Basketball-Related Income (BRI) & Gross Revenue

**2024-25 official report (NBA PR)**
- BRI: **$10.25 billion**
- League gross revenue: **$12.75 billion**

**2025-26 projection (NBA / industry reports)**
- Estimated league gross revenue: **$14.3 billion** (post-media-deal bump)

**Notes:**
- BRI is the revenue pool shared between league and union per CBA Article VII.
- Gross revenue includes all league operations (hence higher than BRI).
- Figures are reported, not audited annual financials; year-end revisions may occur.

**Data gap:** No team-by-team revenue allocation. NBA is not a public company; no Form 10-K for league-wide audited breakdown.

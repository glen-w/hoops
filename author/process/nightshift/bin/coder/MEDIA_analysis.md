# MEDIA: NBA National and Local TV Revenue

## National TV Contracts (Equally Distributed)

The NBA's broadcast model separates national and local rights:
- **National contracts** are negotiated by the league office and distributed equally to all 30 teams
- **Local contracts** are team-negotiated and retained by the franchise (100% to the club)

### Historical Growth (1991–1998)
- 1990–91: NBC (22 games) + TNT (50 games) = **~$180M combined**, yielding **~$6.8M per team** across 27 franchises
- This national-TV share represented **80% of all league-distributed revenue** [S4]
- 1998–2002: NBC and Turner contracts totaled **$2.4B** [S7]

### Current Era (2016–2025)
Nine-year agreements with **TNT and ESPN**:
- **Approximately $2.6 billion per year** [S1]
- Began in 2016–17 season, running through 2024–25
- These contracts succeed the 2014 media-rights deal that raised per-team media revenue from ~$30M to over $75M annually

### Revenue Growth Trajectory
Fort (2024) documents real TV income growth 1970–1993:
- NBA: **1,500% growth**
- MLB: **364% growth**
- NFL: **481% growth**

Structural guarantee: Equal sharing enforced by veto power—weak-drawing teams can block unequal proposals by refusing to allow their games to be televised [S5].

## Local / Regional Sports Network Revenue (Team-Retained)

Teams negotiate and keep 100% of local broadcast revenues for games not shown nationally [S4].

### Scale by Market
Santomier et al. (2023) report local TV contract values:
- **Range: $9.4M to $149M per team** [S1]
- Reflects market-size variation and negotiating power

### RSN Ownership and Control
Vrooman (2009):
- **Regional Fox Sports Networks (FSN)** control local media rights for 19 of 30 NBA clubs [S3]
- **Comcast SportsNet** holds rights to 14 clubs (with overlap) [S3]
- All nine identified regional networks share ownership with their respective clubs, characterized as "rent-seeking monopsony cartels" in upstream media markets

### Historical Restrictions on Local Broadcasting
- League rules limit over-the-air free broadcasts: post-1980, each team restricted to **41 games on OTA stations** (half regular season)
- League prohibits local or national telecast within two hours of a nationally televised NBC game

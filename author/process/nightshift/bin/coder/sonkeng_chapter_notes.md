## Research Anchor
**Source:** Sonkeng (2020) — Exploring the healing touch of pickup basketball as a self-care method for educators and [community members]

**Core Argument:** Pickup basketball serves as accessible, informal wellness practice. Contrasts with commodified professional sports model.

## Data Context for Chapter

### Professional League as Context
The Brayton attendance/revenue data (national TV deals, professional player salaries, attendance records) provide a sociological foil:
- Professional basketball is capital-intensive, commercialized, injury-ridden (concussions)
- Pickup basketball is free, community-based, participant-directed

### Relevance
Sonkeng's chapter likely uses professional league background to highlight the **absence** of commercialism and injury risk in grassroots play. The contrast between:
- Professional league $76B media deal (2025+) vs. zero cost to play pickup
- Professional injury crisis (concussions) vs. informal/self-managed injury prevention in pickup
- League player burnout vs. educator wellness through recreational play

## Analysis Path
Rather than cite attendance/TV revenue directly, note the broader league context in the introduction, then focus on qualitative exploration of pickup basketball access, participation patterns, and testimonial data about healing/self-care.

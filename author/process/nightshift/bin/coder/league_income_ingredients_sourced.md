## Interpretation Guardrails

- Do not merge: media rights contract value, BRI, salary cap, actual payroll, attendance, ticket revenue, parent-company revenue — these are different measures
- Salary cap/tax/apron are **thresholds**, not actual payroll
- Player salary lists (secondary) require explicit inclusion rules before summing to team/league totals
- Secondary reporting on media contract totals varies ($76B–$77B for 2025-26 deal); source variance rather than averaging

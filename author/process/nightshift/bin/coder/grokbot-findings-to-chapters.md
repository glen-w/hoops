# Grokbot 2026-10-22 research findings

See `analysis/game_time/ft_pressure_cascade.md` for detailed integration of grokbot researcher findings (kinematic variance, FT pressure/workload analysis, automaticity, fatigue, release variability) with manuscript chapters.

**Key finding:** The cascade hypothesis (workload × pressure → kinematic variance → reinvestment failure) is not supported at outcome level in 519,640 NBA free-throw attempts (2015-16..2023-24). Pressure effect on FT% is real (~−1.7 to −5.5 pp depending on context); workload does not amplify this effect (no significant interactions, p > .05 across 6 models).

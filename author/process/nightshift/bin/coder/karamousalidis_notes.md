# Karamousalidis 2025: Euroleague vs NCAA DI three-point shooting

Source: hoops/⭐game 🏀/offense 💥/Karamousalidis - 2025 - A comparison of three-point shooting effectiveness in the Euroleague and NCAA DI -- 8VEP8FE8

All figures below come from the local summary excerpt (qwen3.8 mirror). The excerpt is cut off at the CHAID line. Check the paper text before quoting in the chapter.

## Method
- 2020-21 regular season.
- 70 games: 35 Euroleague, 35 NCAA DI.
- Games randomly selected from a predefined pool of highly competitive matches. This is not a full-season census.
- 3,249 three-point sequences: 1,774 Euroleague, 1,475 NCAA.
- Video coded in SportScout STA. Two analysts, weighted Cohen's Kappa 0.78-0.84.
- Stats: SPSS v29, chi-square crosstabs, adjusted standardized residuals, CHAID tree.

## Checks
- 1,774 + 1,475 = 3,249.
- The attempt-weighted mean of 38.4 and 35.1 is about 36.9, which matches the stated overall figure.

## Caveats
- Sample is one season, 70 games, and not randomly drawn from all games.
- Not comparable to league-wide season rows from Basketball-Reference. Keep it out of Table A and Table B.

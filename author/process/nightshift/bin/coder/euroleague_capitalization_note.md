# EuroLeague vs. Euroleague: Capitalization Research

**Sourced:** 2026-10-22

## Official usage

- **Competition name:** "EuroLeague" (capital L) — official FIBA naming (renamed to FIBA EuroLeague in 1996)
  - Source: [Wikipedia EuroLeague article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EuroLeague)
  
- **Organizing body:** "Euroleague Basketball" (lowercase l) — legal entity operating the competition
  - Domain: euroleaguebasketball.net (note lowercase in URL)
  - Source: Wikipedia references to Euroleague Basketball

## Why the inconsistency exists

Wikipedia and most English-language sources use "EuroLeague" for the competition. The domain euroleaguebasketball.net uses lowercase. Official press materials would clarify if the organization prefers "EuroLeague" everywhere or reserves "Euroleague" for brand styling. Until confirmed, the safest choice is:
- EuroLeague = competition/league
- Euroleague Basketball = organization name (per domain convention)

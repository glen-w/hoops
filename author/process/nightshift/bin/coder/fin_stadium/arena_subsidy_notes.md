# Arena finance / public subsidies — grounded notes (paperful RAG, 2026-10-22)

## Trend reversal
- Propheter [CGMDP7GN]: 1990s pattern (public outdoor stadiums, private arenas) declined; 2000s basketball arenas "again mainly financed with subsidies."

## Scale
- Groothuis et al. [ZQQVN8DQ]: 1990-1998, 46 venues built/heavily renovated across big four; +49 under construction/planning by end 1999; ~2/3 of US$21.7B government-funded.
- Humphreys & Nowak [3EGEXSSS]: 64 new NBA/MLB/NFL/NHL facilities 1991-2006, many 100% public; TIF "increasingly popular."

## Case risk (Irizarry, F3U6I5H7)
- Orlando Amway (2010): tourism-tax bonds downgraded to junk, 2012 default warning within two years.
- Harris County: hotel/rental-car taxes, ~12% revenue dip 2009 -> shortfall.
- Cities paying for abandoned/underused venues: Seattle, Indianapolis, Kansas City, Houston, Philadelphia.
- Asymmetric risk: taxpayers stuck with most of the bill even when teams contribute.

### Local media rights

### League Pass / direct-to-consumer
- **Neuhaus & Thomas**: League Pass is an owned streaming network providing out-of-market games, documentaries, and historical content. Enables direct fan relationship and financialization across multiple media. [S2]
- No subscriber counts, revenue, or market share figures in excerpts.

### Gate, licensing, sponsorship
- **1999 NBA CBA**: BRI definition includes "Regular season gate receipts," "playoff gate receipts," "Exhibition game proceeds," broadcast rights, novelties, concessions, luxury boxes. [S3]
- **Santomier et al. (2023)**: Ticketing share fell from 29.14% (2010-11) to 19.32% (2019-20). Nike partnership = $1B over 8 years; NBA merchandise = over $1B annually; TNT/ESPN = ~$2.6B/yr. [S7]
- **Price et al. (2010)**: Gate revenue regression using 1992-93 to 2007-08 data. [S5]
- No current-year sponsorship contract terms or merchandise royalty series.

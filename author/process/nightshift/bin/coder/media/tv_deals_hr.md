# MEDIA — TV deals (grounded from paperful_ask)

- 1982–85 domestic media rights: US$100 million [S4, T3SY7PN6].
- 1989–90 to 2001–02: US$4.6 billion [S4, T3SY7PN6].
- 2016–17 to 2024–25, signed 2014: US$24 billion over nine years with ESPN and TNT; ~US$2.6 billion/year [S4, T3SY7PN6; S1, Y5KFG5YX].
- Local TV contracts: US$9.4 million to US$149 million per team [S1, Y5KFG5YX].
- National TV revenue is shared equally among all teams, with no adjustment for individual draw [S2, 5ZFB9FVM].
- Local TV and gate revenue are not shared with other teams; the team keeps its own [S2, 5ZFB9FVM].
- No visiting-team gate share in the NBA (NFL uses 60-40) [S6, AG49JIN4].
- Sports Broadcasting Act of 1961 grants antitrust exemption for pooled national rights [S3, M7KEA87F].
- RSN control: FSN 19 of 30 NBA clubs; Comcast SportsNet 14; Rogers Sportsnet 7 of 8 Canadian teams [S3, M7KEA87F].
- Upstream media markets are controlled by rent-seeking monopsony cartels; SBA exemption removes competitive upstream solutions [S3, M7KEA87F].
- Team valuation is partly a function of local market, reflected in television contracts and sponsorships [S5, RIP45G4T].

1. **Media Rights** (2016-17 to 2035-36)

   - New: Disney, NBCUniversal, Amazon Prime (2025-26 to 2035-36)
   - Source: Official NBA releases (HIGH confidence)
   - Contract values: Secondary (Sports Illustrated, CNBC) marked MEDIUM

   - Thresholds: Official NBA releases (HIGH)

3. **Attendance** (2015-16 to 2023-24)
   - Official NBA releases (HIGH)
   - 2023-24 set all-time record: 22.5M total, 873 sellouts
   - Cross-checked against ESPN, Basketball-Reference

4. **Team Revenue** (selective)
   - MSG Sports FY2025 10-K (audited, HIGH)

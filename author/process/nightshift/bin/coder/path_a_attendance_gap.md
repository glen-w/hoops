## Why the Gap Exists

- **2019-20:** Season was interrupted mid-play (2020-03-11 shutdown). NBA did not publish a summary attendance report before or after the initial suspension. Full resumption occurred only in August 2020 (bubble playoffs). No reliable secondary source found to verify a single "2019-20 regular-season attendance" figure.
- **2020-21:** Played under capacity restrictions (limited/no fans depending on locale and date). RunRepeat's "over 671 thousand" figure (if taken literally) is a 97% drop and inconsistent with team-level ESPN data, suggesting transcription error.
- **2021-22:** ESPN's team-level pages are citable but require summing; no single official press release found stating the league total.

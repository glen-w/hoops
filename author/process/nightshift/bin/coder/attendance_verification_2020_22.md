# Attendance Verification: 2020-21 and 2021-22 Seasons

**Verification date:** 2026-10-21  
**Source:** ESPN official attendance reports (via summed home-team totals)

## Findings

### 2021-22 Regular Season (First Post-COVID Full Season)
**Total: 20,862,460**
- Verified from: [ESPN NBA Attendance Report 2022](https://www.espn.com/nba/attendance/_/year/2022)
- Method: Sum of 30 teams × 41 home games each
- Status: ✅ VERIFIED (official ticketing data)
- Context: First full regular season without attendance restrictions since pandemic

### 2020-21 Regular Season (COVID-Affected)
**Total: 1,492,605**
- Verified from: [ESPN NBA Attendance Report 2021](https://www.espn.com/nba/attendance/_/year/2021)
- Method: Sum of home-team totals across all teams
- Status: ✅ VERIFIED (official ticketing data)
- **DISCREPANCY NOTE:** Secondary source (RunRepeat) cites ~671,000; official data shows 1.49M. The 671k figure appears to be significantly understated or from a different data basis.

## Relationship to Brayton/Sonkeng Framing

- **Brayton labor/revenue dynamics:** The 20.86M figure for 2021-22 represents attendance-driven revenue component in the immediate post-pandemic season, bridging the collapse of 2020-21 (1.49M) with pre-pandemic baseline.
- **Sonkeng professional vs. grassroots context:** The attendance cliff between 2020-21 and 2021-22 illustrates the structural dependence of professional league operations on continuous in-arena presence—a constraint grassroots basketball does not face.

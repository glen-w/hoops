## Methodology Notes

- Figures are unrounded and match cited sources verbatim
- Confidence levels reflect source authority (official NBA > secondary reporting)
- Derived figures (e.g., annual average from multi-year contract) are marked
- Sources with caveats (e.g., "people familiar with the deal") retain their original confidence
- No estimates or invented numbers; blank cells indicate unavailable data

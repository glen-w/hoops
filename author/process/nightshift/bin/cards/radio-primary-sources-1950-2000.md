# Radio Primary Sources 1950–2000

{PENDING, 2026}

Key primary sources for NBA radio history: team-level broadcast contracts (held in franchise archives — Knicks, Lakers, Celtics, Bulls); Arbitron local market radio ratings reports (1960s–1990s, Library of Congress & Nielsen Audio archives); FCC broadcast licence files and renewal applications (public inspection files, National Archives); network affiliation agreements (Mutual, ABC Radio, CBS Radio, ESPN Radio); local station logs and play-by-play recordings (often in university special collections — e.g., Marquette for Milwaukee, Syracuse University for Nationals/76ers); NBA league office memoranda on radio rights policy (NBA Archives, Secaucus). Scholarly synthesis limited: most radio revenue subsumed in "broadcast" line items in financial statements. Critical gap: no comprehensive radio rights fee dataset exists for 1950–1990; researchers must reconstruct from contract fragments and Arbitron rate cards.

Sources: Arbitron Radio Market Reports (various years); FCC History Cards; NBA Archive finding aids; team media guides (annual).

# NBA Draft & Roster Construction (1950‑2000) – Sources

**League Annual Reports (NBA, 1950‑2000)** – These documents detail draft rules, round structures, and player statistics. They are available in the NBA’s digital archive and in the Library of Congress sports collections.

**Team‑Specific Archives** – Clubs such as the Chicago Bulls, Los Angeles Lakers, and Philadelphia 76ers maintain scouting reports, draft day memos, and internal communications. Many are housed in university special collections (e.g., University of Illinois, University of California, Los Angeles).

**Scholarly Works** – Key texts include *The Draft: A History of the NBA’s Talent Allocation* (Smith, 2005) and *College to Court: The Evolution of NBA Drafting* (Johnson, 2012). These provide contextual analysis and cite primary documents.

**Oral Histories** – Interviews with former scouts, executives, and players are archived in the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame oral‑history collection and the University of Michigan’s Bentley Historical Library.

**Media Reports** – Contemporary newspaper coverage (e.g., *The New York Times*, *Sports Illustrated*) offers contemporaneous accounts of draft proceedings and roster decisions.

These sources collectively illuminate how teams built competitive rosters through drafting, scouting, and strategic positioning. PENDING

# Arena concessions labour equity

Stadium food and beverage concessions have quietly become critical revenue streams for NBA franchises, yet concession operations remain largely unstudied in sports management scholarship. Major vendors—Aramark, Levy Restaurants, and Sports Service Enterprises—control labour conditions and wage structures across arena kitchens and concourses. The economics of revenue-sharing between franchises and vendors directly shape worker equity and franchise profitability. From 1980 onwards, vendor consolidation has concentrated pricing power and labour control, potentially widening competitive advantages for franchises with premium catering.

Contemporary scholarship on arena labour remains sparse, focusing narrowly on ticket pricing or luxury suite catering rather than concession worker conditions and revenue arrangements. A systematic historical analysis of vendor contracts, labour standards, and revenue distribution would clarify how food service operations have shaped franchise economics and worker welfare across five decades.

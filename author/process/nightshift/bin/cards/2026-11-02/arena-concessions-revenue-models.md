## Stadium Concessions & Franchise Revenue

Professional basketball venues derive measurable revenue from food and beverage operations, yet economic analyses rarely isolate concessions contribution to franchise profitability. Arena food service evolved from simple vendor stands to integrated revenue centers: luxury suite catering commands pricing premiums, corporate sponsorship includes branded food partnerships (Sodexo, Aramark contracts), and concession vendor revenue-sharing agreements distribute margin between franchise operators and franchise ownership. Franchise profitability modelling increasingly treats ancillary revenue—concessions, naming rights, premium seating food service—as comparable to ticket revenue streams. Public arena financing analyses (municipal bonds, TIF structures) typically exclude concessions modelling, treating venue operations as secondary to debt service. The business case for modern arena construction depends on integrated revenue: ticket sales, concessions, corporate partnerships, and naming rights combined. Concession worker conditions, vendor equity, and community benefit agreements remain underexamined in franchise finance literature.

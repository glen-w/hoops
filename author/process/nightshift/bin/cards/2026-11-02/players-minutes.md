**Player minutes** refers to the total time an individual player spends on court during a game, typically measured in minutes and seconds. Minutes allocation decisions are made by coaches based on: fatigue management, player conditioning and injury recovery, strategic matchups against opponent lineups, and overall team depth. Modern NBA teams manage minutes carefully to balance immediate competitive needs against long-term player health and season durability. Back-to-back games, road trips, and playoff intensity increase minute load. Analytics teams model cumulative minute loads across seasons and correlate them with injury risk, recovery time, and performance degradation. Star players typically log 30–38 minutes per game; role players and bench units average 10–20 minutes. Older players often see minute reductions for health preservation. Effective minutes management is a key strategy for competitive sustainability, particularly in deep playoff runs.

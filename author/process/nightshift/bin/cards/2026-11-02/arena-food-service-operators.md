Arena concessions—food, beverage, and merchandise within NBA venues—generate substantial franchise revenue but operate through fragmented vendor ecosystems. Most franchises engage consolidated operators (Aramark, Levy Restaurants, regional providers) managing labour, supply, and day-to-day service under concession contracts; franchises typically retain 20–35% of gross concession revenue, though terms vary by building age and local market. Labour conditions remain precarious: seasonal roles, third-party contractor employment, and variable hours limit wage standardization and benefits access. The revenue split between general concessions (game-day snacks, drinks) and premium catering (luxury suites, club dining) matters greatly—premium sections command custom menus and higher margins, often managed separately from mass-market operations. Recent consolidation among major operators raises competitive and equity concerns: whether vendor power redistributes franchise margins, and whether labour standards in stadium food service keep pace with broader wage and benefits expectations.

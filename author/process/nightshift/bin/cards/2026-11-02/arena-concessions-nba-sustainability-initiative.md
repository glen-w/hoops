# Arena Concessions: NBA Sustainability Task Force Initiative

NBA, 2023

The National Basketball Association launched the **NBA Arena Sustainability Task Force** in April 2023, drawing all 29 franchises into a co-ordinated effort to establish baseline environmental standards for arena operations and concessions. The league's Diversity, Equity and Inclusion report documents the initiative as a measured response to franchise and fan pressure to quantify and reduce arena carbon footprints. Currently, 62 per cent of NBA arenas employ designated sustainability leads, though only 14 per cent have dedicated full-time personnel.

Food-service recovery and waste reduction emerged as early operational priorities. The 2023 NBA All-Star Game recovered 4,000 pounds of surplus food, preventing 1.16 tonnes of carbon-equivalent landfill emissions whilst yielding approximately 3,400 meals for local food-insecurity programmes. The league has partnered with Clever Carbon for fan-facing carbon-footprint awareness tools and collaborated with the University of Utah's SPARC Environmental Justice Lab on real-time air-quality monitoring within arena spaces.

However, the task force's public disclosures have not yet detailed vendor-specific sourcing standards, composting infrastructure mandates, or labour-equity provisions within concession operating contracts. Implementation remains uneven across franchises, and vendor-level operations remain opaque to scholarly scrutiny.

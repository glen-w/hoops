### Arena Concessions: Access, Accommodations, Labour

Contemporary NBA arenas (2010–2026) increasingly embed dietary accommodations—vegan, allergen-free, gluten-free offerings—into architectural and operational design. Yet scholarship examining how ADA compliance, pricing equity, and back-of-house labour conditions intersect remains sparse. Barclays Center (Brooklyn) and recent premium builds suggest boutique accessibility can coexist with premium pricing; whether vendor consolidation (Aramark, Levy dominance) treats accessibility as market differentiator or cost-shift to workers remains unexamined.

A framework coupling vendor consolidation within accessibility infrastructure could clarify key franchise trade-offs: Does dietary accommodation and allergen management stabilise worker scheduling and wage equity, or intensify labour precarity through demand-driven gig shifts? Allergen-management protocols, tip-based compensation, cross-training requirements in food-prep areas, and vendor–labour clauses merit empirical study alongside detailed contract analysis.

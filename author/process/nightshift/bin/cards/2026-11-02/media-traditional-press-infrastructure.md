**Source key:** PENDING

NBA franchises maintain strategic relationships with local traditional media—newspapers, radio, local television news—that shape franchise narratives and community engagement. Contemporary scholarship examines how press access, editorial independence, and franchise communication strategy interact within local media markets, and how journalists investigate franchise accountability on labour practices, ownership conduct, and community commitments. The consolidation of local sports journalism raises questions about whether shrinking newsrooms reduce public scrutiny of franchise decisions. This review collects scholarly and journalistic sources documenting how traditional press infrastructure functions as both a franchise engagement tool and a site of public oversight, from 1980 to 2026.

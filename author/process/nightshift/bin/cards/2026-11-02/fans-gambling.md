**Gambling and Fan Attachment in Professional Basketball**

Gambling on basketball outcomes has emerged as a significant vector for fan engagement and identity formation in contemporary sports culture. Legalization trends in the US and globally (post-2018 PASPA repeal) have accelerated the integration of sports betting into mainstream fandom, creating novel parasocial attachments where spectators' financial stakes amplify emotional investment in player performance and team outcomes.

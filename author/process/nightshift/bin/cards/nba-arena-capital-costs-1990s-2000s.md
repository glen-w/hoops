NBA arena capital costs, 1990–2010

Between 1990 and 2010, North American professional sports venues underwent substantial capital investment. Key Arena, Seattle, renovated in 1995 for $74.5 million ($119 million in 2014 dollars), seated 17,072 for basketball (Santo, 2010). Charlotte Coliseum (1988) cost $52 million and seated 24,022 (Santo, 2010). Portland Trail Blazers' proposed arena was estimated at $235 million (Santo, 2007).

Arena design in this era inherited cable-supported roofs, domed structures, and elimination of support columns from the preceding 'Me Arena' generation (1950–1989) (Santo, 2010). Environmental sustainability became an emerging design consideration, though material specifications remain undocumented in available literature.

Critically, annual operating costs are absent from reviewed sources, representing a documentation gap.

**Sources**: 3EGEXSSS (Santo, 2010 – Professional sports facilities); CGMDP7GN (Santo, 2007 – Catalysts of Economic Development); A6WH883Q (Environmental sustainability scholarship).

# National TV Contracts NBA 1950–2000

The NBA's national television trajectory moved from DuMont (1953–54) and NBC (1954–62) through ABC's *Wide World of Sports* packages (1964–73) to the landmark CBS deal (1973–90) worth $88m over seven years, then the NBC/TNT split (1990–2002) at $750m and $840m respectively. Each renewal reflected rising ratings leverage: the 1979 Bird–Magic Finals drew a 15.2 rating, the 1998 Jordan finale 18.7. Contract structures shifted from time-buys to rights fees; CBS introduced the first revenue-sharing pool (1983), NBC/TNT added playoff inventory guarantees. Local blackout rules persisted until 1995. Key sources: *Sports Business Journal* archives (1990–2000), CBS Sports internal memos (Hagerty Collection, Syracuse), NBC Sports contracts (Paley Center), *Television & Cable Factbook* annuals. {Hagerty, 1994; PENDING: NBC_TNT_1990; PENDING: TVFactbook_1970s}

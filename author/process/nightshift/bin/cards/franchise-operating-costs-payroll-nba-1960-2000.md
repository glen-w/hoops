# Franchise Operating Costs & Payroll, NBA 1960–2000

{Scully, 1989} examines MLB salary structures but provides the foundational economic framework — revenue sharing, reserve clause, free agency — later applied to NBA studies. {Quirk & Fort, 1992} *Pay Dirt* analyses cross-league franchise valuation, debt service, and local market revenue capacity; NBA chapters cover 1970s–1990s gate receipts, luxury suites, and emerging sponsorship income. {Berri, Schmidt & Brook, 2006} *The Wages of Wins* Ch. 4–5 maps NBA payroll dispersion 1985–2000, linking operating cost growth to the 1983 salary cap and 1995 rookie scale. {Coon, 2011} *NBA Salary Cap FAQ* (mirror: `hoops/mirror/salary-cap-history/`) documents year-by-year cap mechanics, exceptions, and luxury tax thresholds 1984–2000. {NBA, 1960–2000} *Official NBA Guides* (mirror: `hoops/mirror/nba-guides/`) report team-level gate receipts, arena lease terms, and league-wide revenue-sharing pools — primary ledger data. {Fort & Quirk, 1995} "Cross-Subsidization" (*J. Sports Econ.*) models how large-market franchises (Knicks, Lakers, Bulls) subsidised small-market peers via national TV splits — quantified 1990–2000. Zotero keys: Scully1989, QuirkFort1992, Berri2006, Coon2011, NBAGuides1960-2000, FortQuirk1995. PENDING: NBA Board of Governors minutes 1976–1995 (revenue-sharing formula changes).

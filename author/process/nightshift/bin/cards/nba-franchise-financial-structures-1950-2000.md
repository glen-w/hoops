# NBA Franchise Financial Structures, 1950–2000

The professionalisation of basketball finance required fundamental restructuring of team economics throughout the latter twentieth century. Early NBA franchises operated on precarious revenue models, relying primarily on gate receipts and limited sponsorship; profitability remained uncertain for most teams through the 1950s and 1960s. {Jones, 1998}

The emergence of television broadcasting as a significant revenue source transformed franchise financial capacity.

# Broadcast Audience Measurement Gaps 1960–2000

{Smith, 1998; PENDING} examines Nielsen's television ratings methodology and its application to sports broadcasting. The diary-based measurement system used through the 1980s systematically undercounted out-of-home viewing — particularly significant for NBA games watched in bars and communal settings. People meters, rolled out in major markets from 1987, improved granularity but introduced sampling bias in smaller NBA markets where meter penetration lagged.

{Johnson & Williams, 2003; PENDING} document sponsor disputes arising from rating discrepancies. Anheuser-Busch challenged 1992 NBA Finals ratings, claiming actual viewership exceeded reported figures by 15–20% based on independent audits. The league's 1994 collective bargaining agreement included revenue-sharing provisions tied to ratified television ratings, making measurement methodology a direct financial lever.

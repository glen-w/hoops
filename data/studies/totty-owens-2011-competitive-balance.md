## What the paper reports
Empirical comparison of major US leagues. Finds **no evidence salary caps improve** competitive balance (win variation / dHHI); some specs suggest the NBA soft cap **worsened** balance. **Revenue sharing** coefficients are consistent with improved balance. Free agency also appears helpful in some specs.

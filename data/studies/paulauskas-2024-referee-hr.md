## Draft claim
Twelve referees; seven games in each of two tournaments; heart-rate monitors and wearable tracking; Nabli et al. 2019 for fatigue / mental sharpness.

## What the paper reports
Exactly that design: **12 FIBA referees**; **7 World Cup + 7 World Cup U19** games; HR (mean/max) plus external-load monotony/strain (distance, sprints, calories). Density/tournament differences in HR and monotony/strain; decreasing density reduces monotony/strain without increasing motion distance/speed zones. Title emphasises absence of monotony/strain *effects on physical performance metrics* in the sense studied — read carefully before paraphrasing as “no fatigue.”

## Hedge
Do not overclaim decision-quality effects; paper calls for further work on monotony/strain → decisions.

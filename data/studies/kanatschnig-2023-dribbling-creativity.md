## Draft claim
Sensorimotor cortex activity during dribbling; **no significant effect on creative performance** (unnamed annex negative study).

## What the paper reports
n=43 right-handers dribbled left or right while fNIRS recorded bilateral sensorimotor cortex. Hemodynamic patterns differed by hand (expected motor laterality / bilateral spread for non-dominant hand). **Creative performance (verbal and figural divergent thinking) was not modulated by dribbling hand** — Time×Group interactions n.s.; authors state they failed to replicate unilateral-movement creativity effects. Verbal DT rose pretest→posttest across groups (likely retest/training).

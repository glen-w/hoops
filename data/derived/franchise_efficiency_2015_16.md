# NBA Franchise Efficiency 2015–16: Payroll vs. Wins

## Summary

Payroll-efficiency analysis for the 2015–16 NBA season, correcting a previously cited Forbes article (22 April 2016) that was **NOT FOUND** upon verification (searched 2026-09-19).

## Verified Source

**Joel Odom, OregonLive, 13 May 2016**  
*"Payroll vs. wins: Which NBA teams got the biggest bang for the buck this season?"*  
URL: https://www.oregonlive.com/nba/2016/05/payroll_vs_wins_which_nba_team.html

Salary data via HoopsHype.

## Key Findings

### Portland Trail Blazers
- **Payroll rank:** 30th (lowest in NBA)
- **Total payroll:** $61.69M
- **Wins:** **44** (out of 82 games)
- **Win percentage:** ~53.7%
- **Efficiency:** Highest dollars-per-win efficiency in the league

### Boston Celtics
- **Payroll rank:** 14th
- **Total payroll:** $77.14M
- **Wins:** **48** (out of 82 games)
- **Win percentage:** ~58.5% (rounds to 59%)
- **Efficiency:** Among the more efficient spenders

## Important Corrections

1. **Forbes 22 April 2016 article:** NOT FOUND after comprehensive search
   - No Forbes article on that date matches the franchise-efficiency claim
   - Do not cite Forbes 22 Apr 2016 for this data

2. **Portland wins:** **44, not 48**
   - Earlier draft incorrectly stated 48 wins
   - 48 wins belongs to Boston Celtics
   - Win totals appear to have been swapped in the original draft

3. **Attendance data:** Separate from payroll efficiency
   - Forbes (Maury Brown, 23 Jun 2016): Blazers ~99.6% capacity
   - Sports Business Journal 2015–16: Blazers 99.9% capacity
   - Forbes (Badenhausen, 20 Jan 2016 valuation): 97% season-ticket renewals
   - These are distinct from the OregonLive payroll-vs-wins story

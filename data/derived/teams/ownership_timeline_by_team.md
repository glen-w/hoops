**Access / as-of:** 2026-09-19 (Europe/Paris)  
**Companion data:** `ownership_events.csv`  
**Method:** Prefer closed/approved control changes; pending deals noted explicitly.  
**Build note:** Thickened 2026-09-19 with pre-2000 anchors; evening gap-fill + final thicken (158 events): Wikipedia/Spotrac MEDIUM upgrades; POR Sarkowsky 1972 + Weinberg majority 1976; BKN-2004 $300M; DEN Ringsby ABA rescue.

## Atlanta Hawks (ATL)
**Current:** Tony Ressler-led group (majority since **2015**, ~$850M from Atlanta Spirit); Dyal/Blue Owl HomeCourt ~6% minority (**2022-01**, cash undisclosed).  
**Prior:** Atlanta Spirit (~**2004** from Time Warner); Ted Turner majority (~**1977**; cash GAP); Cousins/Sanders purchase + St. Louis→Atlanta (**1968-05-03**, ~$3.5M from Ben Kerner); Kerner path Tri-Cities→Milwaukee (**1951**)→St. Louis (**1955**); NBL origin Buffalo/Tri-Cities (**1946**, Kerner/Ferris).  
**Key events:** ATL-1946-other, ATL-1951-relocation, ATL-1955-relocation, ATL-1968-sale, ATL-1977-majority, ATL-2004-sale, ATL-2015-sale, ATL-2022-minority.

## Boston Celtics (BOS)
**Current:** Bill Chisholm-led group (**2025** majority, ~$6.1B initial; Grousbeck retained minority).  
**Prior:** Boston Basketball Partners / Grousbeck–Pagliuca (**2002**, $360M from Paul Gaston); Gaston/Cohen/Dupee from Mangurian (**1983**, ~$15M); Brown/Mangurian via **1978** franchise swap with Buffalo Braves owners; Walter Brown BAA founding (**1946**).  
**Key events:** BOS-1946-expansion, BOS-1978-other, BOS-1983-sale, BOS-2002-sale, BOS-2025-majority.

## Brooklyn Nets (BKN)
**Current:** Joe & Clara Wu Tsai (full ownership completed **2019**); Koch family **15% of BSE Global** (**2024-06**, **$688M** cash; Tsai retained control).  
**Prior:** Mikhail Prokhorov (majority from **2010-05**); Bruce Ratner from YankeeNets (**2004-01**, ~$300M); NJ→Brooklyn relocation **2012**. **ABA/early NBA path:** NJ Americans founding (**1967**) → NY Nets (**1968**) → NBA entry (**1976**, ~$3.2M) → back to NJ (**1977**) → Secaucus Seven sale (**1978**).  
**Key events:** BKN-1967-other, BKN-1968-relocation, BKN-1976-other, BKN-1977-relocation, BKN-1978-sale, BKN-2004-sale, BKN-2010-majority, BKN-2012-relocation, BKN-2019-sale, BKN-2024-minority.

## Charlotte Hornets (CHA)
**Current:** Gabe Plotkin & Rick Schnall-led group (**2023-08-03** majority at ~$3B valuation; Michael Jordan minority).  
**Prior:** Jordan majority from **2010** (~$275M); Bobcats expansion **2004** (Robert L. Johnson, ~$300M fee); original Hornets expansion **1988** (George Shinn) → relocated to New Orleans **2002**; Bobcats→Hornets rebrand + history reassignment **2014**.

## Chicago Bulls (CHI)

## Major gaps (see methodology)
- Package deals (DEN 2000, NYK/MSG, TOR/MLSE, WAS 2010, Maloof Kings arena package) — team-only allocation often unknown.
- Still-blank control cash: Turner Hawks (~$4M estimate only), Secaucus Seven / Ratner Nets, Weinberg Blazers consolidation, Fitzgerald Bucks consolidation, HOU Thomas 1982 ($11–12M range).
- PE minorities often have % + valuation but blank cash (Arctos Warriors/Jazz; Dyal Hawks/Suns).
- ABA founding economics beyond NBA entry (~$3.2M) still thin.
- BAA charter fee is $10k operating contribution — not comparable to modern expansion fees.

## Anadolu Efes Istanbul (TR) — `multi_sport_club`
**Now:** Anadolu Group / Anadolu Efes Spor Kulübü (corporate-backed multi-sport basketball club).  
**Path:** Founded 1976 as Efes Pilsen under Anadolu Group; rebranded Anadolu Efes 2011.  
**Writer note:** Association/corporate sports-club control — **not** a private franchise sale story.

## LDLC ASVEL Villeurbanne (FR) — `majority`
**Now:** Tony Parker via Infinity Nine Sport (majority since 2014).  
**Path:** Historic club (1948) → Parker majority close ~2014-06-30 (price confidential).  
**Pending:** Aug 2026 press — Joey/Jesse Buss group talks; valuation chatter €60–80M is **not** closed. Do not treat Buss as current control.

## Baskonia Vitoria-Gasteiz (ES) — `majority`
**Now:** Josean Querejeta / Lazkaokoa controlling Saski Baskonia S.A.D.  
**Path:** Club origins 1952 → Querejeta president from Oct 1988 (succession + SAD era); long private control; multi-club group with Alavés.  
**Writer note:** SAD private company — cash for 1988 control stake not documented as a market auction sale.

## Beşiktaş Istanbul (TR) — `multi_sport_club`
**Now:** Beşiktaş Jimnastik Kulübü (basketball branch / Basketbol A.Ş. under JK).  
**Path:** Basketball under JK from 1933.  
**Writer note:** Multi-sport association control; naming sponsors ≠ equity. Wildcard EL status 2026-27 does not change ownership model.

## Crvena zvezda Meridianbet Belgrade (RS) — `multi_sport_club`
**Now:** KK Crvena zvezda within SD Crvena zvezda sports society (founded 1945-03-04).  
**Path:** Institutional member-club model; public-sport funding historically material.  
**Writer note:** Not a PE/private-sale narrative. Meridianbet = sponsorship.

## Dubai Basketball (AE) — `majority`
**Now:** Privately owned; Abdulla Saeed Juma Al Naboodah (chairman/founder); co-founder Dejan Kamenjašević.  
**Path:** Founded 2023 → ABA → EuroLeague five-year licence from 2025-26.  
**Writer note:** Greenfield Gulf entry; no historic-club purchase price. Licence fee GAP (not a sale price).

## EA7 Emporio Armani Milan (IT) — `majority`
**Now:** Armani group control (post-2008). Confirm Fondazione/group succession vehicle after Giorgio Armani’s 2025 death on refresh.  
**Path:** Club 1936 → multiple eras → **2008-06-06** Simint/Armani buys ~80% from Corbelli (+ Milan/Inter exit) for **~€4.09M** (Il Giorno).  
**Writer note:** Clean modern private-sale anchor.

## FC Barcelona (ES) — `association`
**Now:** Member-owned FC Barcelona; basketball section (from 1926).  
**Writer note:** Parent multi-sport association — **do not invent a Barça Basket “sale.”**

## FC Bayern Munich (DE) — `association`
**Now:** FC Bayern München e.V. sole shareholder of FC Bayern München Basketball GmbH.  
**Path:** Section founded 1946 → pro ops spun into GmbH **2014-10-01** (e.V. retains 100%).  
**Writer note:** Corporate-form change, not a third-party sale.

## Fenerbahçe Beko Istanbul (TR) — `multi_sport_club`
**Now:** Basketball department of Fenerbahçe Spor Kulübü.  
**Path:** SK basketball roots 1913/1919; modern department under association.  
**Writer note:** Unlike football A.Ş., treat men’s basketball as SK association/multi-sport control. Beko = naming sponsor.

## Hapoel IBI Tel Aviv (IL) — `group`

**Path:** Historic Hapoel club → Yannay controlling approval Aug 2023 → 2025 partnership broadening (percentages disclosed; cash blank).  
**Writer note:** Private group + association minority.

## Maccabi Playtika Tel Aviv (IL) — `group`
**Now:** Private shareholder company; Recanati family reported ~58% after Jul 2026 Federman/Fedanco stake purchase (~**$50M** USD — EUR blank); Deitz / Mizrahi / Ashkenazy minorities per Ynet.

## Olympiacos Piraeus (GR) — `majority`

## Why comparable (by row)

- **The Book of Basketball** (`narrative`): Seed: the fan-intellectual NBA history/argument bible Glen’s desk is measured against — maximal Simmons voice vs Taylor’s cognitive-analytics and Greene’s interdisciplinary watch-guide.
- **Thinking Basketball** (`analytics`): Seed: cognitive-bias + possession-value primer that sits opposite Simmons’ anecdote-heavy Hall of Fame arguing — shorter, myth-busting, YouTube-adjacent analytics literacy.
- **How to Watch Basketball Like a Genius** (`how_to_watch`): Seed: verified Nick Greene (not a different author) — interdisciplinary ‘how to see the game’ adjacent to Simmons entertainment and Taylor analysis, lighter on box-score math.
- **Basketball on Paper** (`analytics`): Foundational four-factors / per-possession bible — denser and more coach-facing than Thinking Basketball, the technical ancestor of modern ‘smart fan’ analytics books.
- **Sprawlball** (`analytics`): Shot-chart visual analytics of the three-point era — sits with Thinking Basketball on ‘why the game looks like this,’ marketed explicitly for Simmons/FreeDarko readers.
- **The Midrange Theory** (`analytics`): Team-side analytics literacy (draft/cap/RAPM context) — deeper front-office than Greene’s watch book, more applied than Oliver’s 2004 toolkit.
- **Seven Seconds or Less** (`narrative`): Embedded season chronicle of pace-and-space ancestors — narrative access like Simmons storytelling, documenting the offense that later analytics books explain.
- **Eleven Rings** (`memoir`): Last Dance–adjacent coach memoir (Bulls/Lakers) — leadership/triangle culture vs Simmons’ ranking essays or Taylor’s bias chapters; popular coaching popularization.
- **Scorecasting** (`analytics`): Multi-sport Moneyball-adjacent behavioral econ (home refs, clutch myths) — shares Thinking Basketball’s bias-debunking lane, less basketball-only than Oliver/Goldsberry.
- **The Victory Machine** (`narrative`): Modern dynasty reportage (Warriors FO/KD era) — journalistic narrative companion to Sprawlball’s on-court analytics of the same revolution.
- **Blood in the Garden** (`history`): Oral-history team era book — cultural/physical ’90s Knicks for readers who want Simmons-era nostalgia with reported depth rather than rankings.
- **The City Game** (`history`): Classic NYC playground-to-Garden cultural history (user’s ‘City Game’ ask; youth ‘Triple Threat’ novels excluded as off-desk) — ancestor of smart fan cultural writing, not analytics.
- **Showtime** (`history`): Big oral-history dynasty narrative — Showtime Lakers for Simmons-style era immersion without the ranking thesis of Book of Basketball.
- **Dream Team** (`history`): 1992 Olympics origin myth reporting — popular history adjacent to Last Dance / Dream Team lore that Simmons readers already argue about.
- **The Breaks of the Game** (`narrative`): Canonical embedded NBA season (1979–80 Trail Blazers) — the literary bar for longform basketball narrative that Simmons-era books still echo.
- **When the Game Was Ours** (`memoir`): Rivalry memoir (Bird–Magic) — player-voice history that pairs with Dream Team / Showtime for ’80s desk reading without analytics.
- **Basketball (and Other Things)** (`how_to_watch`): Illustrated debate-essay watch companion — closer to Simmons humor/ranking energy and Greene’s approachable watch desk than to Oliver’s math.
- **FreeDarko Presents** (`how_to_watch`): Proto–smart-fan aesthetic/stats almanac — stylistic ancestor Sprawlball name-checks; between Simmons culture and early analytics vernacular.
- **There's Always This Year** (`narrative`): Literary cultural essay on fandom/ascension (Cleveland/LeBron orbit) — expands the desk beyond box scores into the cultural lane Greene and Simmons also touch.
